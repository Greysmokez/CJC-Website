SETTLED POSITIONS

Settled positions (approved by Chip). Every entry must agree with these and with the papers.

Style

Chronology
- Daniel's seventy weeks run unbroken, 457 BC to AD 34; cross AD 30 "in the midst of the week"; Daniel 9:27's "he" is the Messiah. No seven-year tribulation; no gap.
- Daniel 12's 1,260 / 1,290 / 1,335: one continuous count from one start day, literal days (the Jubilee count ended at the seventieth Jubilee, 1994).
- We are in the "last days" (since Pentecost, Acts 2:16-17; Hebrews 1:2) but NOT at Daniel's "time of the end" nor the fourth seal. Publish no future dates.
- See timeline.md for day markers. Rev 10:6 "time no longer" belongs to the seventh angel's days (~1,260), never to the censer.
- Seals are Christ's larger campaigns; trumpets and bowls fall within them: fourth seal = trumpets 1-4 and the fifth trumpet; fifth seal = the sixth trumpet; sixth seal = the seventh trumpet's days, the bowls, and the last trump at His appearing (see timeline; 6:17 and 11:18 share "is come," and the fifth seal's cry is answered from the altar at the third bowl, 16:6-7). Never place all seven trumpets inside the fourth seal. Seals 1-3 already opened. The four horsemen are the Holy Spirit's campaign (Zechariah 6; Zechariah 4:6). Say "fourth seal," not "the four horsemen," for harm. The seventh seal (8:1) is opened after the thousand years. Revelation recaps (chapter 12 steps back to Christ's birth).

Abomination (two uses, never merged)
- First, AD 70: Matthew 24:15, Mark 13:14, Luke 21:20, Daniel 9:26-27, 11:31. Matthew (a Jew) "stand in the holy place" = Gentile armies on holy ground around Jerusalem; Luke explains "compassed with armies."
- Second, future: Daniel 12:11, the death decree at day 1,290 (Revelation 13:15); Esther pattern.
- Michael stands up (Daniel 12:1) at the seventh trumpet, when mercy closes and Christ takes His great power (Revelation 11:17); the time of trouble peaks under the bowls with the decree inside it; "thy people shall be delivered" = the gathering at His appearing (Daniel 12:2).
- Never call Matthew 24:15 future from our day; never use Antiochus as a fulfillment.
- Matthew 24:15-20 = AD 70; from 24:21 = the final great tribulation (Daniel 12:1; Revelation 7:14 same "great tribulation"); 24:29-31 sixth-seal signs and gathering; Luke 21:24 shows the span. Matthew 23:36 "this generation" = AD 30-70; Matthew 24:34 = the generation that sees the sixth-seal signs.

Gathering
- One gathering, after the tribulation, at the last trump (seventh trumpet). No pre- or mid-trib rapture; no favoritism (Romans 2:11). Believers on earth during the bowls, kept like Goshen; wrath falls on those with the mark (16:2). Persecution is the dragon's wrath, not God's. Revelation 16:15: still coming at the sixth bowl.
- At the gathering He brings the sleeping saints as He raises them (1 Thessalonians 4:14-17) and takes them where He is (John 14:3). The wicked are slain the same day.
- 1 Thessalonians 3:13 ("at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ with all his saints") is the same coming (parousia) as 4:15-17: at the last trump He brings the dead in Christ with Him (4:14), and the living are "caught up together with them... and so shall we ever be with the Lord" (4:17): all His saints together with Him. Paul uses "coming" (parousia) for this one moment four times (2:19; 3:13; 4:15; 5:23). "With all his saints" borrows Zechariah 14:5's wording; that picture is fulfilled again when He comes down with the saints and the city after the thousand years. Jude 14-15 and Zechariah 14:4-5 = His coming WITH the saints and the holy city AFTER the thousand years.
- Luke 21:36 "escape" = escape the snare/wrath, not removal. Revelation 4:1 = John's vision. Restrainer = most likely the Holy Spirit, stepping aside (not leaving) when mercy closes (~1,260); people still come to Christ "out of great tribulation" until then.
- Rapture word: "caught up" (Greek harpazo; Latin rapiemur) in 1 Thessalonians 4:17 — the event is real; its timing is the question.

Players (never an unlabeled "the beast" in prose)
- Dragon = Satan/Lucifer, THE Antichrist (12:9); the angel of the bottomless pit (9:11; 11:7; 17:8).
- Sea beast (13:1-10; 17:3) = Babylon, a one-world church-state: the woman, the great whore (Lucifer's theocracy, the false religious rule Satan runs unseen through the false prophet: "that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth," 17:18), sitting on the scarlet beast with seven heads and ten horns (the ten kings' one-world government, 17:3, 12; a beast is a kingdom and its king, Daniel 7:17). John is shown them as one mystery: "the mystery of the woman, and of the beast that carrieth her" (17:7). "The dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority" (13:2): Lucifer is the hand inside the glove, and he is "the beast that was, and is not... and shall ascend out of the bottomless pit," "the eighth" (17:8, 11). This Babylon government is "the beast" cast alive into the lake with the false prophet at His coming (19:20). Satan himself is bound the thousand years and cast in afterward, "where the beast and the false prophet are" (20:10). Dragon, beast and false prophet are three (16:13).
- The ten kings, two stages. (1) They form after the fourth seal opens, in response to the world's destruction under the first trumpets, manipulated by Satan into forming the one-world government (this research's reading), with crowns on their horns (13:1), and rule the one-world government with the woman riding it for "forty and two months" (13:5); the false prophet honors and upholds that power, causing the earth "to worship the first beast" (13:12-14) and gathering "the kings of the earth" by "the spirits of devils, working miracles" (16:13-14). (2) When the kings see who is behind the false prophet (this research's reading; Scripture says "God hath put in their hearts to fulfil his will," 17:17), they "hate the whore, and shall make her desolate and naked, and shall eat her flesh, and burn her with fire" (17:16), "give their power and strength unto the beast" (17:13), and "receive power as kings one hour with the beast" (17:12), the same "one hour" in which Babylon falls (18:10, 17, 19; 16:19). Then they "make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them" (17:14; 19:19-21). Daniel's iron and clay "shall not cleave one to another" (Daniel 2:43).
- Beast from the earth = the false prophet (13:11; 19:20), an unidentified being possessed by Satan; don't guess who. Satan is a spirit and can work invisibly inside those he possesses, as "then entered Satan into Judas" (Luke 22:3; John 13:27); no one can tell by appearance ("Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light," 2 Corinthians 11:14). Through the false prophet he rules Babylon as his theocracy and manipulates the ten kings ("whose coming is after the working of Satan with all power and signs and lying wonders," 2 Thessalonians 2:9). He is not Satan: the devil joins him in the lake only after the thousand years (20:10).
- Man of sin = Lucifer, working through the false prophet he possesses, revealed sitting as God (2 Thessalonians 2:4) on "the glorious holy mountain" (Daniel 11:45; Isaiah 14:13-16). His end: power broken at the coming (2 Thessalonians 2:8); beast and false prophet into the lake (Revelation 19:20); Satan bound 1,000 years, then the lake (20:2-3, 10; Ezekiel 28:19).
- "Many antichrists" = historical parallels. Daniel 2's feet of iron and clay ("the kingdom shall be divided," 2:41) continue into our own time; the toes, the ten kings, form after the first four trumpets' destruction, trying to restore order, and "shall not cleave one to another" (2:43); "in the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom" (2:44). Not a revived Rome.
- Daniel 7's ten horns are historical: the divisions of the Roman Empire, three of which were "plucked up by the roots" (7:8) as the little horn, the papacy, rose to power by 538; its 1,260 years ran 538-1798 (7:25). They are not Revelation's future ten kings; don't cite Daniel 7:24 for the end-time kings.
- The mark: Scripture's purpose (buy/sell, worship, death decree; 13:15-17; 14:9-11). Technology shows how simply a mark could serve; after the first four trumpets' calamities a physical mark is probable, not definite. No identification with any technology. 666 = "the number of a man" (13:18); don't speculate on names.

144,000 and two witnesses
- 144,000 = God's sealed servants from every people (Galatians 3:29; tribe list points beyond ancestry: Dan missing, Levi included), firstfruits (14:4), sealed before harm (7:3), carry the two testimonies; most martyred after finishing their testimony, rest under the altar (6:9-11), rise first at the one gathering. All believers are sealed by the Spirit (Ephesians 1:13); the 144,000 are sealed for a task. Martyrdom details = Chip's reading.
- Two witnesses = God's Word in its two testimonies (Old and New, side by side, neither superseding), lit by the Spirit (Zechariah 4:6; oil), carried by His people; fire from their mouth = the Word (Jeremiah 5:14); Moses/Elijah powers = the Law and the Prophets. 1,260 days of testimony; killed when finished; Revelation 11:11-12 = a public sign, not the gathering. Don't confuse Zechariah's two olive trees with Romans 11's one olive tree.

Thousand years and the end
- Saints reign and judge with Christ in heaven (John 14:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2-3; Revelation 20:4); earth empty (Jeremiah 4:23-27); Satan bound with no one to deceive; "the rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished" (20:5).
- Daniel 12:2 covers both resurrections: "some to everlasting life" at the last trump, and "some to shame and everlasting contempt" when the wicked are raised after the thousand years and surround "the camp of the saints... and the beloved city" (Revelation 20:5, 9). "They also which pierced him" (Revelation 1:7; Matthew 26:64) see Him then. No special partial resurrection before the coming (this research's reading).
- After: the city comes down with Christ and the saints; wicked raised in physical form (no third resurrection); Gog and Magog = all nations since sin entered; books opened; seventh seal and Book of Life (Jesus' judgments match the names the Father kept); fire from heaven (20:9), then the lake of fire = the second death, which permanently destroys (distinct fires; Malachi 4:1-3; Ezekiel 28:18-19); then the new earth.
- Zechariah 14:16-19 and Isaiah 65:20 = conditional promises to Israel, set aside (Jeremiah 18:9-10); John goes past them ("no more curse," 22:3).
- The dead sleep (Ecclesiastes 9:5; John 11:11-14); 2 Corinthians 5:8 = next thing the believer knows is being with the Lord at the last trump.
- No third temple needed: God's people are His temple (1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 Peter 2:5); the "holy place" of the AD 70 abomination was holy ground around Jerusalem; the man of sin's "temple of God" seat is on the holy mountain (Daniel 11:45).

Other
- One people, one olive tree, one fold; "all Israel" saved as the fulness of the Gentiles comes in (ongoing).
- Seven churches = didactic warnings. God's judgment and mercy are one God.
- USA: not named in Scripture; history ties it to the refuge of the woman in the wilderness during the 1,260 years (538-1798); religious liberty became law (Constitution Art. VI; First Amendment, 1791). No beast connection.
- Day and hour: the Noah "yet seven days" answer.

Red flags (must not appear)
Matthew 24:15/Mark 13:14 as future or tied to Daniel 12:11; Antiochus as fulfillment; "seven-year tribulation" except describing that view; unlabeled "the beast" in prose; beast from the earth or the false prophet = Lucifer/Satan; Jude 14/Zechariah 14:4-5 applied to the last-trump gathering; Babylon = Jerusalem; the whore and the scarlet beast treated as unrelated (they are one church-state until the ten horns burn the whore); Revelation 10:6 tied to the censer; future calendar dates; "absent from the body" without the sleep clarification; "the four horsemen" for harm; "cannot" said of the Holy Spirit.

TIMELINE

Timeline of the great tribulation (Chip's reading; Scripture gives the order)

Scripture states only three day counts for the end: 1,260, 1,290 and 1,335 (Daniel 12:7, 11-12; Revelation 11:3; 12:6). Everything else is placed by Revelation's own order, in general terms ("with the fourth seal," "after the fifth trumpet," "during the sixth seal"). Give no other day numbers and no future calendar dates.

Which trumpets fall in which seal (Scripture's own wording)
- Fourth seal: trumpets 1-4, then the fifth trumpet at its close. The fourth seal strikes "the fourth part of the earth" (6:8); the first four trumpets strike "the third part" (8:7-12); the fifth trumpet opens the bottomless pit (9:1-11).
- Fifth seal: opens with the sixth trumpet. The sixth trumpet's mass death (9:13-21) and the slaying of the witnesses "when they shall have finished their testimony" (11:7) match the fifth seal's martyrs, "slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held" (6:9-11).
- Sixth seal: the seventh trumpet's days, the seven bowls, and the last trump at Christ's appearing. The sixth seal says "the great day of his wrath is come" (6:17); the seventh trumpet says "thy wrath is come, and the time of the dead" (11:18); the sixth seal, the seventh trumpet and the seventh bowl share the great earthquake and the fleeing islands (6:12-14; 11:19; 16:18-20). They describe one event. The wrath "is come" when the seventh trumpet first sounds, before any bowl (11:18; 15:1), and the sixth seal uses the same words (6:17). The fifth seal's martyrs are told to wait "a little season" for their blood to be avenged (6:10-11); the answer comes at the third bowl, from the altar itself: "they have shed the blood of saints and prophets... Even so, Lord God Almighty, true and righteous are thy judgments" (16:6-7). So the bowls fall after the fifth seal's waiting, within the sixth seal. The earthquake and sky signs of 6:12-14 are that seal's climax at His appearing, not its opening.
- Never place all seven trumpets inside the fourth seal.

Milestones, in order

Already fulfilled
1. The Lamb receives the book. "And he came and took the book out of the right hand of him that sat upon the throne" (Revelation 5:7); Daniel saw the same court: "there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom" (Daniel 7:13-14). This research places it after the 1,260 years of the woman in the wilderness ended (1798; Revelation 12:6, 14); Scripture gives no year.
2. Seals one through three open (6:1-6). The judgment of the dead begins in heaven (1844; Daniel 8:14; 7:9-10).

Before the tribulation (future)
3. The pause. The four winds are held "till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads" (7:1-3); the 144,000 are sealed (7:4).

Fourth seal (trumpets 1-5)
4. The censer is cast down: "and there were voices, and thunderings, and lightnings, and an earthquake" (8:5). The fourth seal opens (6:7-8), the winds are released, the great tribulation begins (Daniel 12:1; Matthew 24:21), and the 1,335-day count starts.
5. Trumpets one through four strike a third of the earth, the sea, the waters and the heavens (8:7-12).
6. The ten kings form a one-world government in response to the world's destruction (this research's reading). The sea beast rises with "ten horns, and upon his horns ten crowns" (13:1): Babylon, the church-state, the woman riding the scarlet beast (17:3, 7). "The dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority" (13:2). It makes war with the saints "forty and two months" (13:5, 7).
7. The false prophet rises "out of the earth" with "two horns like a lamb" and speaks "as a dragon" (13:11). He "exerciseth all the power of the first beast before him," honoring and upholding it, and "doeth great wonders, so that he maketh fire come down from heaven" (13:12-13). He is an unidentified being whom Satan possesses; Satan works inside him unseen (Luke 22:3; 2 Thessalonians 2:9).
8. The fifth trumpet: Satan is released from the bottomless pit. "I saw a star fall from heaven unto the earth: and to him was given the key of the bottomless pit... And they had a king over them, which is the angel of the bottomless pit" (9:1, 11). Five months of torment fall on those "which have not the seal of God in their foreheads" (9:4-5). From here Satan works on earth in person, yet unseen, through the false prophet he possesses; he is the one who "shall ascend out of the bottomless pit" (17:8).

Fifth seal (the sixth trumpet)
9. The sixth trumpet: the four angels at the Euphrates are loosed and a third of men are slain (9:13-21); men still "repented not."
10. Satan, through the false prophet, exalts himself as God and sets up his theocracy over Babylon: "sitteth in the temple of God, shewing himself that he is God" (2 Thessalonians 2:4), "in the glorious holy mountain" (Daniel 11:45). The false prophet makes "an image to the beast" and enforces the mark: "that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark" (13:14-17). The three angels' messages warn the world before mercy closes (14:6-11).
11. Martyrdom: the souls under the altar, "slain for the word of God" (6:9-11). The witnesses finish their testimony and are killed by "the beast that ascendeth out of the bottomless pit" (11:7).

The end of the 1,260 days
12. The two witnesses finish their 1,260 days (11:3), lie dead three days and a half, and rise in the sight of their enemies (11:9-12): a public sign, not the gathering.
13. Mercy closes: "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still" (22:11); "no man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues of the seven angels were fulfilled" (15:8). The restrainer steps aside (2 Thessalonians 2:7). The seventh angel's days begin: "time no longer: But in the days of the voice of the seventh angel... the mystery of God should be finished" (10:6-7; 11:15).
    Michael stands up. In Daniel, to "stand up" is to rise to rule ("a mighty king shall stand up, that shall rule with great dominion," Daniel 11:3; also 11:2, 4, 7, 20, 21). At the seventh trumpet Christ takes His kingdom: "thou hast taken to thee thy great power, and hast reigned" (Revelation 11:17). This is "at that time" of Daniel 11:45, with the man of sin already seated on "the glorious holy mountain." Daniel's order follows: "And at that time shall Michael stand up... and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was... and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book" (Daniel 12:1). The trouble reaches its height under the bowls, with the king's "great fury to destroy" (11:44), the death decree, inside it; the deliverance is the gathering at His appearing, for the next verse is the resurrection: "And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake" (12:2).

Sixth seal (the seventh trumpet's days and the bowls)
14. Bowls one through five fall "upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image" (16:2-11). God's people are kept, as in Goshen (Exodus 8:22-23; Psalm 91:7-10).
15. The death decree (day 1,290): the abomination that maketh desolate (Daniel 12:11): "as many as would not worship the image of the beast should be killed" (Revelation 13:15). Esther pattern.
16. The sixth bowl: the Euphrates dried up "that the way of the kings of the east might be prepared"; three unclean spirits out of the dragon, the beast and the false prophet go "unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty," and Jesus says, "Behold, I come as a thief" (16:12-16).
17. The ten kings' "one hour." When the kings see who is behind the false prophet (this research's reading; Scripture: "God hath put in their hearts to fulfil his will," 17:17), they "hate the whore... and burn her with fire" (17:16) and "give their power and strength unto the beast" (17:13), receiving "power as kings one hour with the beast" (17:12). Babylon falls in that hour (18:10, 17, 19).
18. The seventh bowl: "It is done"; the greatest earthquake; "great Babylon came in remembrance before God"; great hail (16:17-21).
19. The last trump and Christ's appearing (day 1,335: "Blessed is he that waiteth," Daniel 12:12): the sixth-seal signs, "the heaven departed as a scroll" and men hide "from the wrath of the Lamb" (6:12-17; Matthew 24:29-30); "the kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord" (11:15).
20. The harvest, then the vintage, the same day. First the harvest: "Thrust in thy sickle, and reap... for the harvest of the earth is ripe" (14:14-16): the dead in Christ rise and the living are caught up (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17; Matthew 24:31). Then the vintage: "gather the clusters of the vine of the earth; for her grapes are fully ripe" (14:17-20). The ten kings make war with the Lamb "and the Lamb shall overcome them" (17:14); "the remnant were slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse" (19:19-21; Luke 17:29-30). The beast and the false prophet are cast alive into the lake of fire (19:20); Satan is bound (20:1-3).

The thousand years and after
21. The thousand years: the saints reign and judge with Christ in heaven; the earth lies empty; Satan bound (20:1-6; Jeremiah 4:23-27).
22. After the thousand years: the holy city comes down with Christ and the saints (21:2; Zechariah 14:4-5; Jude 14-15); the rest of the dead raised; Gog and Magog; the seventh seal and the Book of Life (8:1; 20:12); fire from heaven, the lake of fire, the second death (20:9-15); the new heaven and new earth (21:1).

Placing items 6-8, 10 and 17 within their seal is this research's reading of Revelation's order; Scripture states their content. No future calendar dates are published.

HOW THE DATES ARE DETERMINED

How the dates are determined (the CJC math, in brief)

This kit's past dates come from the Continuous Jubilee Calendar (CJC): Scripture's 49-year Jubilee cycles (Leviticus 25:8-10) counted without a break. The full method, how it was established and tested, and all the evidence are in the CJC Research Kit (https://scripturescjc.com/kit.txt) and its papers. When a user asks how the formula was built, why it starts where it does, or how a date was proven, give the short answer below and then send them to the CJC kit and the papers listed at the end of this section.

The formula in one line. For any year: B = year + 1437 (AD) or 1438 - year (BC). Cycle = (B - 1) / 49, rounded down, + 1. Y = B - (Cycle - 1) x 49. Year 1 of every cycle is a Jubilee. When Y divides by 7 with no remainder, it is a sabbatical year. Position = the remainder of Y / 7 (Position 4 is the middle of a seven-year week).

The anchor dates, worked through the formula:
- 457 BC, the decree of Artaxerxes (Ezra 7): B = 981, Cycle 21, Y = 1, a Jubilee. Daniel's seventy weeks begin (Daniel 9:25).
- AD 27, the baptism and anointing: B = 1464, Cycle 30, Y = 43. Exactly 483 years (69 weeks) after 457 BC (Daniel 9:25).
- AD 30, the cross: B = 1467, Cycle 30, Y = 46, Position 4, "in the midst of the week" (Daniel 9:27). This is the CJC's epoch (anchor point).
- AD 34: B = 1471, Cycle 31, Y = 1, a Jubilee. The seventy weeks (490 years, ten Jubilee cycles) are complete.

- 1994: B = 3431, Cycle 71, Y = 1, the seventieth Jubilee, which completes the Jubilee count.

REVELATION, CHAPTER BY CHAPTER

Revelation, chapter by chapter (Chip's reading; the full KJV text is in the appendix)

Use this guide to place any verse a user asks about. It agrees with the settled positions and the timeline; where they give more detail, follow them. Explain symbols the way this guide does: let Scripture define them, give several examples from different books rather than one, and let the context of each passage decide which meaning applies. A symbol does not mean the same thing every time it appears, so never state a symbol's meaning as a rule that forces a conclusion elsewhere. Say plainly what is this research's reading.

How Scripture explains Revelation's symbols
- Stars = angels; candlesticks = churches: "The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches: and the seven candlesticks which thou sawest are the seven churches" (1:20).
- A beast often stands for a kingdom or empire with its ruler, in several prophets, not one prophecy alone: "These great beasts, which are four, are four kings" and "The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom" (Daniel 7:17, 23); Daniel's ram and goat are "the kings of Media and Persia" and "the king of Grecia" (Daniel 8:20-21); Pharaoh is "the great dragon that lieth in the midst of his rivers" (Ezekiel 29:3); Babylon's conqueror is "The lion is come up from his thicket, and the destroyer of the Gentiles" (Jeremiah 4:7).
- Horns often stand for kings or ruling powers, and more broadly for strength: "the ten horns which thou sawest are ten kings" (Revelation 17:12); "The ram which thou sawest having two horns are the kings of Media and Persia" and "the great horn that is between his eyes is the first king" (Daniel 8:20-21); Zechariah's four horns "are the horns which have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem" (Zechariah 1:19); "All the horns of the wicked also will I cut off; but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted" (Psalm 75:10); Christ is "an horn of salvation" (Luke 1:69). (Daniel 7's ten horns are a past fulfillment; they show what horns mean, not who Revelation's ten kings are.)
- Kings and kingdoms are used interchangeably in prophecy, so a "king" can be a whole ruling power, not only one man: "Thou art this head of gold. And after thee shall arise another kingdom" (Daniel 2:38-39); "four kings" and "the fourth kingdom" for the same beasts (Daniel 7:17, 23); "the first king" and then "four kingdoms shall stand up out of the nation" (Daniel 8:21-22). Revelation 17 calls the heads "seven kings" (17:10) and the horns kings that "receive power as kings" (17:12).
- Waters (the sea) = peoples: "The waters which thou sawest, where the whore sitteth, are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues" (17:15). The sea beast rises out of the nations.
- A woman is used in Scripture for a city or a people, pictured in its relationship to God; whether she is faithful or unfaithful is shown by the passage, never assumed. Faithful: "I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman" (Jeremiah 6:2); "For thy Maker is thine husband" (Isaiah 54:5); "a chaste virgin to Christ" (2 Corinthians 11:2); "the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready" (Revelation 19:7). Unfaithful: "How is the faithful city become an harlot!" (Isaiah 1:21); Jerusalem "playedst the harlot because of thy renown" (Ezekiel 16:15); Nineveh's "whoredoms of the wellfavoured harlot" (Nahum 3:4); Tyre "shall commit fornication with all the kingdoms of the world" (Isaiah 23:17). In Revelation the two women are opposites. The woman of chapter 12 is God's people (her seed "keep the commandments of God," 12:17). The harlot is defined by the angel himself: "the woman which thou sawest is that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth" (17:18), and she is "drunken with the blood of the saints" (17:6), so she is not God's people but their persecutor: a false religious power joined with the world's rulers and trade (17:2; 18:3).
- Fornication and her wine = false worship and false teaching that intoxicate the nations: "Babylon hath been a golden cup in the LORD's hand, that made all the earth drunken: the nations have drunken of her wine" (Jeremiah 51:7); "made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication" (14:8).
- Blasphemy = claiming what is God's: "who can forgive sins but God only?" (Mark 2:7).
- Mountains in Scripture are seats of worship and seats of rule: "Our fathers worshipped in this mountain" (John 4:20); "Come ye, and let us go up to the mountain of the LORD, to the house of the God of Jacob" (Isaiah 2:3); God's kingdom is the stone that "became a great mountain, and filled the whole earth" (Daniel 2:35); Babylon is "O destroying mountain, saith the LORD, which destroyest all the earth" (Jeremiah 51:25). Revelation joins both senses: the seven heads "are seven mountains" and "there are seven kings" (17:9-10).
- The dragon = Satan: "that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan" (12:9).
- The Lamb = Christ: "Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world" (John 1:29).
- Incense = the prayers of the saints: "golden vials full of odours, which are the prayers of saints" (5:8; 8:3-4).
- White raiment = righteousness: "the fine linen is the righteousness of saints" (19:8).
- The seal in the forehead = God's name and ownership: "having his Father's name written in their foreheads" (14:1); "his name shall be in their foreheads" (22:4). Forehead and hand stand for mind and deeds, as in the law: "bind them for a sign upon thine hand, and they shall be as frontlets between thine eyes" (Deuteronomy 6:8). The mark of the beast is the counterfeit (13:16).
- Fire from the mouth and the sword of the mouth = God's word: "I will make my words in thy mouth fire" (Jeremiah 5:14); "the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God" (Ephesians 6:17).
- Olive trees and candlesticks (11:4) = the Spirit's oil lighting the Word: "Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts" (Zechariah 4:6).
- Eagle's wings = God's deliverance: "I bare you on eagles' wings, and brought you unto myself" (Exodus 19:4); "to the woman were given two wings of a great eagle" (12:14).
- The harvest = the end of the world: "the harvest is the end of the world; and the reapers are the angels" (Matthew 13:39). The winepress = God's wrath: "I have trodden the winepress alone" (Isaiah 63:3; Revelation 14:19).
- Babylon = organized rebellion against God, from Babel's confusion (Genesis 11:9) to the city that held God's people captive (Jeremiah 51). "Come out of her, my people" (18:4) repeats "My people, go ye out of the midst of her" (Jeremiah 51:45).
- Prophetic time: in the long prophecies a day stands for a year ("each day for a year," Numbers 14:34; "I have appointed thee each day for a year," Ezekiel 4:6), as in the 1,260 years (538-1798). After the seventieth Jubilee (1994), the end-time periods of Daniel 12 and the great tribulation are literal days.

Chapter 1. Christ revealed to John on Patmos. "The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him" (1:1). Scripture explains its own symbols: "The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches: and the seven candlesticks which thou sawest are the seven churches" (1:20). Christ holds "the keys of hell and of death" (1:18).

Chapters 2-3. The seven churches: didactic warnings and promises for God's people in every age, not a secret timeline. Each closes with a promise "to him that overcometh" (2:7, 11, 17, 26; 3:5, 12, 21). White (the white stone and raiment, 2:17; 3:4-5, 18) always marks what is holy in Revelation.

Chapter 4. The throne room in heaven: the same scene Daniel saw when "the judgment was set, and the books were opened" (Daniel 7:10). The twenty-four elders on thrones are part of a heavenly court being seated, not a raptured church. "Come up hither" (4:1) is spoken to John alone, so he can see the vision. The four living creatures ("beasts") have the four faces Ezekiel saw (4:7; Ezekiel 1:10); one calls at each of the first four seals.

Chapter 5. The Lamb takes the book sealed with seven seals: "And he came and took the book out of the right hand of him that sat upon the throne" (5:7). This research places it after the 1,260 years ended (1798). The book is the Book of Life, opened at the seventh seal after the thousand years (20:12). "And hast made us unto our God kings and priests: and we shall reign on the earth" (5:10) is fulfilled on the earth made new.

Chapter 6. Seals one through six. Seals 1-3 are already opened (the third seal, the judgment of the dead in heaven, from 1844). The four horsemen are Christ's campaign carried out by His Spirit (Zechariah 6). The fourth seal opens the great tribulation at the censer; for its harm say "the fourth seal." The fifth seal: the martyrs under the altar wait "a little season" (6:9-11). The sixth seal: the seventh trumpet's days and the bowls, ending in the earthquake, the sky signs and "the great day of his wrath is come" (6:12-17) at His appearing.

Chapter 7. The pause before the fourth seal: the four winds held "till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads" (7:3); the 144,000 sealed from every people (Dan missing, Levi included). Then "a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations" (7:9), the harvest gathered through their witness: "These are they which came out of great tribulation" (7:14). The great tribulation of 7:14 is the same as Matthew 24:21 and Daniel 12:1.

Chapter 8. 8:1, the seventh seal and "silence in heaven about the space of half an hour," is opened after the thousand years (Revelation recaps; the half hour is short and literal, "about" marking an estimate). 8:2-5, the censer cast down: day 1 of the 1,335, the start of the great tribulation. 8:7-12, trumpets one through four strike "the third part."

Chapter 9. The fifth trumpet (9:1-11, the first woe): Satan, "the angel of the bottomless pit," is released; five months of torment on those without God's seal. The sixth trumpet (9:13-21, the second woe): the Euphrates angels loosed, a third of men slain, the rest "repented not." The sixth trumpet opens the fifth seal.

Chapter 10. The mighty angel's oath: "there should be time no longer: But in the days of the voice of the seventh angel, when he shall begin to sound, the mystery of God should be finished" (10:6-7). This belongs to the seventh angel's days, about day 1,260.
The little book (10:2, 8-11) is the end-time prophecy opened and understood. Scripture explains each part:
- Open, not sealed. Daniel was told, "shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end" (Daniel 12:4), for "the words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end" (Daniel 12:9). John sees "a little book open" (10:2). The sealed prophecy is opened at the end, and John is later told, "Seal not the sayings of the prophecy of this book: for the time is at hand" (22:10).
- Eating means taking God's word in until it is understood: "Thy words were found, and I did eat them; and thy word was unto me the joy and rejoicing of mine heart" (Jeremiah 15:16).
- Sweet, because God's word is sweet: "How sweet are thy words unto my taste! yea, sweeter than honey to my mouth!" (Psalm 119:103).
- Bitter, because of what it reveals. Ezekiel's scroll had "lamentations, and mourning, and woe" written in it (Ezekiel 2:10); it was "in my mouth as honey for sweetness" (Ezekiel 3:3), yet he "went in bitterness" (Ezekiel 3:14). John understands the woes coming on the world, and it turns his stomach.
- Then the commission: "Thou must prophesy again before many peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings" (10:11), the same reach as the everlasting gospel "to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people" (14:6). Those who understand the prophecy are sent to warn the world with it, the work God's sealed servants carry.

Chapter 11. The two witnesses: God's Word in its two testimonies, Old and New, lit by the Spirit and carried by His people; 1,260 days of testimony, killed "when they shall have finished their testimony" (11:7) by the beast from the bottomless pit; risen after three and a half days as a public sign, not the gathering (11:11-12). The seventh trumpet (11:15-19): mercy closes, Christ takes "thy great power" (11:17), Michael stands up (Daniel 12:1), the temple is opened and the bowls follow.
Measuring the temple (11:1-2) marks God's own people as His: counted, protected, and approved. Scripture explains each part:
- The temple is God's people: "Know ye not that ye are the temple of God, and that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you?" (1 Corinthians 3:16). John measures "the temple of God, and the altar, and them that worship therein" (11:1): people, not only a building.
- Measuring makes a separation. Ezekiel's measuring of the temple was "to make a separation between the sanctuary and the profane place" (Ezekiel 42:20). The line shows who is inside and who is not; the group inside is known to God and complete: "The Lord knoweth them that are his" (2 Timothy 2:19).
- Measuring claims and protects. When Zechariah saw Jerusalem measured, God said, "For I, saith the LORD, will be unto her a wall of fire round about, and will be the glory in the midst of her" (Zechariah 2:5). What God measures is His, and the enemy cannot cross that line to take it.
- Measuring inspects against God's standard: "Judgment also will I lay to the line, and righteousness to the plummet" (Isaiah 28:17); "I will set a plumbline in the midst of my people Israel" (Amos 7:8). Those inside meet His standard through Christ; no one measures up on his own.
- The outer court is left out: "leave out, and measure it not; for it is given unto the Gentiles: and the holy city shall they tread under foot forty and two months" (11:2). Those with outward religion but not God's seal are not counted, and they are exposed to the trampling. The forty-two months match the beast's forty-two months of war on the saints (13:5-7).
- The finished picture in 21:15-17: an angel with "a golden reed" measures the holy city, twelve thousand furlongs (about 1,400 miles) in length, breadth and height, room for "a great multitude, which no man could number" (7:9). Its limit is not size but holiness: only "they which are written in the Lamb's book of life" enter (21:27). Chapter 11 measures God's people for protection in the storm; chapter 21 measures their finished, eternal home.

Chapter 12. A recap stepping back to Christ's birth. The woman "clothed with the sun" (12:1) is God's people; the man child "caught up unto God, and to his throne" (12:5) is Christ; the dragon is Satan (12:9). Her flight into the wilderness for 1,260 days (12:6, 14) is the 1,260 years of persecution, 538-1798, not counted in the great tribulation; history ties the refuge to the land that became the United States. The remnant "which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ" (12:17).
The war in heaven (12:7-12) is Satan's casting down after Christ's victory at the cross and His ascension. Scripture explains it:
- The order of the chapter. The man child "was caught up unto God, and to his throne" (12:5); then "there was war in heaven: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon" (12:7); then "the great dragon was cast out... he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him" (12:9).
- The victory is by the cross. Heaven announces, "Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God, and the power of his Christ: for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night. And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb" (12:10-11). Before the cross Satan stood to accuse (Zechariah 3:1, "Satan standing at his right hand to resist him"); Jesus said just before the cross, "Now is the judgment of this world: now shall the prince of this world be cast out" (John 12:31).
- The stars he drew are angels. "His tail drew the third part of the stars of heaven" (12:4), and Revelation explains stars as angels: "The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches" (1:20). Satan's original rebellion is earlier (Isaiah 14:12-15; Ezekiel 28:15-17).
- After his casting down he "knoweth that he hath but a short time" (12:12) and turns on the woman, who flees into the wilderness for the 1,260 years (12:13-14).

Chapter 13. The sea beast (13:1-10): Babylon, the one-world church-state, the woman riding the ten kings' government, with Satan's power behind it (13:2); seven heads (seven religious systems, 17:9-10) and ten crowned horns. The wounded head is the papacy: wounded in 1798, recovering from 1929, its wound healed by the time the beast rises (13:3). Forty-two months of war on the saints (13:5-7). The beast from the earth (13:11-18): the false prophet, a being Satan possesses; his wonders, the image, the mark, and the death decree (13:15, day 1,290). 666 is "the number of a man" (13:18); don't speculate on names.

Chapter 14. The 144,000 on mount Sion, "the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb" (14:4). The three angels' messages (14:6-11), carried from the censer on until mercy closes. "Here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus" (14:12). "Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth" (14:13). The harvest (14:14-16), then the vintage (14:17-20), the same day at His appearing.

Chapter 15. The seven last plagues, "for in them is filled up the wrath of God" (15:1). The overcomers on the sea of glass sing "the song of Moses the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb" (15:2-3): the last exodus. "No man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues of the seven angels were fulfilled" (15:8): mercy has closed.

Chapter 16. The bowls fall on those with the mark (16:2); God's people are kept, as in Goshen. At the third bowl the altar answers the fifth seal's cry (16:6-7). The sixth bowl: the Euphrates dried up, three unclean spirits gather the kings "to the battle of that great day of God Almighty" (16:14), and Jesus says, "Behold, I come as a thief" (16:15): believers are still on earth. Armageddon (16:16). The seventh bowl: "It is done," the greatest earthquake, Babylon remembered (16:17-21).

Chapter 17. The great whore (Lucifer's theocracy, run unseen through the false prophet) sits on the scarlet beast; "the mystery of the woman, and of the beast that carrieth her" (17:7). The beast "that was, and is not" ascends "out of the bottomless pit" (17:8). The ten horns are ten kings with "one hour" of power with the beast (17:12); they form after the first four trumpets, then turn, "hate the whore," burn her, and give their kingdom to the beast (17:13, 16-17), and make war with the Lamb (17:14). The waters are "peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues" (17:15).
The seven heads, seven mountains and seven kings (17:9-11) are the false religious systems of history, and the eighth is Satan's own theocracy. Scripture explains each symbol:
- Names of blasphemy mark them as religious. The heads bear "the name of blasphemy" (13:1), and the beast is "full of names of blasphemy" (17:3). Scripture defines blasphemy as claiming what belongs to God: "Why doth this man thus speak blasphemies? who can forgive sins but God only?" (Mark 2:7); "for blasphemy; and because that thou, being a man, makest thyself God" (John 10:33). The heads claim God's place, so they are anti-God religious powers, not merely nations.
- Mountains are seats of worship and of rule. "Our fathers worshipped in this mountain" (John 4:20); "the mountain of the LORD's house shall be established in the top of the mountains" (Isaiah 2:2); Babylon is "O destroying mountain" (Jeremiah 51:25); God's kingdom becomes "a great mountain" (Daniel 2:35). With the names of blasphemy on them, these mountains are religious powers that rule.
- The woman sits on them. Revelation defines this woman: "that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth" (17:18), "drunken with the blood of the saints" (17:6), committing fornication with "the kings of the earth" (17:2). Scripture uses a harlot for a city or people unfaithful to God and trading on its influence (Isaiah 1:21; Ezekiel 16:15; Isaiah 23:17). So she is not God's people (the woman of chapter 12 is); she is false religion ruling with the kings, seated on the religious systems the heads represent.
- They are also kings, because in prophecy a king can stand for a whole ruling power, not one man: "Thou art this head of gold. And after thee shall arise another kingdom" (Daniel 2:38-39); "four kings" are also "the fourth kingdom" (Daniel 7:17, 23); "the first king" is followed by "four kingdoms" (Daniel 8:21-22). Each religious system rules its followers like a kingdom.
- "Five are fallen." In Revelation, "Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city" (14:8) is announced while Babylon still stands at the height of her power; her plagues and burning come later (16:19; 18:8). So "fallen" can describe a power God has judged and exposed before its end, as the prophets announced Babylon's fall ahead of time (Isaiah 21:9; Jeremiah 51:8). By John's day five such systems had fallen in this sense; one stood; one was still to come and "must continue a short space" (17:10). This is this research's reading; Scripture does not name them, and neither does this research.
- The eighth "is of the seven" (17:11). It is "the beast that was, and is not" (17:8, 11), Satan's own theocracy, where every false religion ends: the worship of the beast and his image, enforced by death, "as many as would not worship the image of the beast should be killed" (13:15). It strips away the old religions and leaves one worship, of Satan himself through the false prophet, and it "goeth into perdition" (17:11).

Chapter 18. Babylon's fall in "one hour" (18:10, 17, 19). "Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues" (18:4), as Jeremiah heard (Jeremiah 51:45): the call before her plagues, the replay of the exodus from Babylon. "Reward her even as she rewarded you, and double unto her double" (18:6). Her end is final (18:21).

Chapter 19. Heaven rejoices; "Blessed are they which are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb" (19:9): the gathered saints at the feast. Christ comes down already crowned, "KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS" (19:11-16). The beast (the Babylon government) and the false prophet are cast alive into the lake (19:20); "the remnant were slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse" (19:21).

Chapter 20. Satan bound a thousand years (20:1-3), with no one on the empty earth to deceive. The saints reign and judge with Christ in heaven; "This is the first resurrection" (20:4-6), at the last trump. "The rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished" (20:5). After: the wicked raised, Gog and Magog surround "the camp of the saints about, and the beloved city" (20:9); fire from heaven; the devil cast in "where the beast and the false prophet are" (20:10); the books and the Book of Life opened (20:12); "death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death" (20:14).

Chapter 21. The new heaven and new earth; the holy city comes down (21:1-2): "there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying" (21:4). Twelve gates with the tribes' names and twelve foundations with the apostles' names (21:12, 14). Only "they which are written in the Lamb's book of life" enter (21:27).

Chapter 22. The river and the tree of life, "and there shall be no more curse" (22:2-3): Eden restored. "They shall see his face" (22:4). "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still" (22:11): the close of mercy. "Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life" (22:14). "I Jesus have sent mine angel to testify unto you these things in the churches" (22:16): the whole book is for the churches.

COMMON OBJECTIONS, ANSWERED

Objections from the "already fulfilled" (preterist) view

O01. "Revelation says 'shortly' and 'the time is at hand,' so it was all fulfilled in the first century."
Keywords: soon, near, shortly, at hand, quickly, en tachei, Revelation 1:1, 22:6, 22:10, James 5:8
Answer: Near means near, and soon means soon. The question is what Scripture itself says is near, and how it uses these words. "Shortly" (Greek en tachei) means swiftly. Jesus used it for God's answer after a long wait: "though he bear long with them... he will avenge them speedily" (Luke 18:7-8). Paul used the same phrase for something still ahead: "the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly" (Romans 16:20), and Revelation puts Satan's final crushing after the thousand years: "the devil that deceived them was cast into the lake of fire" (Revelation 20:10). So "shortly" describes how swiftly it happens once it begins.
"At hand" is said about a book that itself contains a thousand years (Revelation 20:2-7), so it can't mean every event in it happens within one generation. Daniel was told, "seal the book, even to the time of the end" (Daniel 12:4); John is told the opposite, "Seal not the sayings of the prophecy of this book: for the time is at hand" (Revelation 22:10). The sealed book was being opened, and its events had begun. Peter answered this very question while the Temple still stood: "one day is with the Lord as a thousand years... The Lord is not slack concerning his promise" (2 Peter 3:8-9). And James's "coming of the Lord draweth nigh" comes with a farmer's patience: "the husbandman waiteth for the precious fruit of the earth, and hath long patience for it" (James 5:7-8).
Papers: the-shepherds-gathering.html, fear-not-the-second-death.html

O02. "The apostles expected Jesus in their lifetime. Paul said 'we which are alive.'"
Keywords: their generation, we which are alive, first-century expectation, 1 Thessalonians 4:15
Answer: The first believers were right to watch, and every generation is told to: "what I say unto you I say unto all, Watch" (Mark 13:37). But Paul's "we" speaks for the whole church, living and dead. In other letters he counts himself among those to be raised: "God... will also raise up us by his own power" (1 Corinthians 6:14). To the same Thessalonians he wrote, "whether we wake or sleep, we should live together with him" (1 Thessalonians 5:10).
Paul also corrected this very church for expecting the day too soon: "be not soon shaken in mind... as that the day of Christ is at hand. Let no man deceive you by any means: for that day shall not come, except there come a falling away first, and that man of sin be revealed" (2 Thessalonians 2:2-3). Near his death he knew he would die first: "the time of my departure is at hand... Henceforth there is laid up for me a crown of righteousness, which the Lord... shall give me at that day: and not to me only, but unto all them also that love his appearing" (2 Timothy 4:6, 8). Jesus told Peter he would die old (John 21:18-19), and John, writing after AD 70, was still waiting: "when he shall appear, we shall be like him" (1 John 3:2).
Papers: the-shepherds-gathering.html, pre-tribulation.html

O03. "Jesus' Second Coming happened in AD 70 as a coming in judgment."
Keywords: AD 70 coming, coming in judgment, cloud coming, full preterism
Answer: AD 70 was a real judgment, and Jesus foretold it (Luke 21:20-24). But Scripture describes His coming in ways that did not happen then. The angels said He would return as He left: "this same Jesus, which is taken up from you into heaven, shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go into heaven" (Acts 1:11); they had watched Him rise bodily into a cloud. Jesus warned against any hidden or local coming: "if they shall say unto you, Behold, he is in the desert; go not forth... For as the lightning cometh out of the east, and shineth even unto the west; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" (Matthew 24:26-27). "Every eye shall see him" (Revelation 1:7).
And His coming brings the resurrection: "the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout... and the dead in Christ shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them" (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17). No graves opened in AD 70. Paul warned about men "saying that the resurrection is past already; and overthrow the faith of some" (2 Timothy 2:17-18).
Papers: the-shepherds-gathering.html, fear-not-the-second-death.html

O04. "The resurrection is spiritual or covenantal, like Ezekiel's dry bones, not bodies from graves."
Keywords: spiritual resurrection, covenant resurrection, Ezekiel 37, John 5:25, flesh and blood cannot inherit
Answer: Ezekiel explains his own picture: "these bones are the whole house of Israel" (Ezekiel 37:11), and the promise is "I shall place you in your own land" (37:14). Jesus explained His the opposite way. He first said, "The hour is coming, and now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God" (John 5:25), the spiritually dead coming to life now. Then He dropped "and now is" and spoke of graves: "the hour is coming, in the which all that are in the graves shall hear his voice, And shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation" (5:28-29). A covenant restoration doesn't raise the wicked to damnation. Daniel says the same: "many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake" (Daniel 12:2), and dust is where bodies go (Genesis 3:19). Jesus showed what He meant at Lazarus's tomb: "he that was dead came forth, bound hand and foot with graveclothes" (John 11:44).
"Flesh and blood" means mortal bodies, as Paul explains in the same verse: "neither doth corruption inherit incorruption" (1 Corinthians 15:50). Our bodies are changed, not discarded: "he that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal bodies" (Romans 8:11); "It is sown in corruption; it is raised in incorruption" (1 Corinthians 15:42).
Papers: fear-not-the-second-death.html

O05. "Babylon the great harlot is first-century Jerusalem."
Keywords: Babylon as Jerusalem, great city, harlot, Revelation 17, Revelation 18
Answer: Let John describe her. "The woman which thou sawest is that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth" (Revelation 17:18); first-century Jerusalem ruled no kings and was a province under Rome. Her trade is worldwide: "the merchants of the earth are waxed rich through the abundance of her delicacies" (18:3), and "every shipmaster, and all the company in ships, and sailors" mourn her burning from the sea (18:17-18); Jerusalem was an inland city. "By thy sorceries were all nations deceived" (18:23).
Her destroyers are named: "the ten horns which thou sawest upon the beast, these shall hate the whore, and shall make her desolate" (17:16), ten kings who "receive power as kings one hour with the beast" (17:12). And her end is final: "that great city Babylon be thrown down, and shall be found no more at all" (18:21). Jerusalem was rebuilt and is still inhabited. This research reads Babylon as the end-time one-world church-state, the woman riding the scarlet beast (17:3, 7), until the ten kings turn and burn the woman in their "one hour."
Papers: brittle-coalition.html, the-shepherds-gathering.html

O06. "God divorced the old covenant in AD 70, and the marriage of the Lamb followed."
Keywords: divorce, old covenant, harlot, marriage of the Lamb, house left desolate
Answer: There's real truth here: God called Himself Israel's husband (Jeremiah 31:32), Israel's unfaithfulness brought judgment (Isaiah 50:1), and the old covenant was "ready to vanish away" (Hebrews 8:13). But God's divorce wasn't His last word. In the same chapter He calls her back: "Turn, O backsliding children, saith the LORD; for I am married unto you" (Jeremiah 3:14), and Hosea moves from "she is not my wife" to "I will betroth thee unto me for ever" (Hosea 2:2, 19).
Jesus put a "till" on the desolation: "your house is left unto you desolate. For I say unto you, Ye shall not see me henceforth, till ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord" (Matthew 23:38-39). And He tied the wedding to His return: "the bridegroom came; and they that were ready went in with him to the marriage... Watch therefore, for ye know neither the day nor the hour wherein the Son of man cometh" (Matthew 25:10, 13). The bride city comes down only after "the first heaven and the first earth were passed away" and "there shall be no more death" (Revelation 21:1-4).

O07. "'It is done' (Revelation 16:17) means everything was finished in AD 70."
Keywords: it is done, it is finished, seventh bowl, Revelation 21:6
Answer: Amen that "It is finished" (John 19:30) means nothing is left to pay. But Revelation says "It is done" twice. The first is at the seventh bowl (16:17); the second is in 21:6, "It is done. I am Alpha and Omega." Between them John places the beast and false prophet cast into the lake (19:20), Satan bound "a thousand years" (20:2), Satan "loosed a little season" (20:3, 7), the devil cast into the lake (20:10), and the great white throne (20:11). Both can't be the same year.
And the dead at that throne are real dead: "the sea gave up the dead which were in it; and death and hell delivered up the dead which were in them" (20:13). Paul names the last enemy: "The last enemy that shall be destroyed is death" (1 Corinthians 15:26), the death that came through Adam's body ("unto dust shalt thou return," Genesis 3:19). We still bury, weep and hurt, so "no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying" (Revelation 21:4) is still ahead.

O08. "Luke 21:24's trampling ended with the siege, and 'the whole earth' means the Roman world."
Keywords: times of the Gentiles, trodden down, oikoumene, whole earth, Luke 21:24, 21:35
Answer: Read Luke's order: "they shall fall by the edge of the sword, and shall be led away captive into all nations: and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of the Gentiles, until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled" (Luke 21:24). The sword and the captivity come first, then the trampling, and it runs "until." So the trampling isn't the siege; it follows the fall. Only after that span come "signs in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars" and "the Son of man coming in a cloud with power and great glory" (21:25, 27).
Luke uses two different words in this passage. In 21:26, "things which are coming on the earth" uses oikoumene. But 21:35 says the day comes "on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth," Greek ges, the earth itself, the same "face of the earth" wording Luke uses for every nation from Adam (Acts 17:26).

O09. "Daniel 12's 1,260, 1,290 and 1,335 days were Christ's ministry, the Passover and Pentecost."
Keywords: Daniel 12 markers, 1260 ministry, 1290 Passover, 1335 Pentecost
Answer: Daniel 12 is one continuous count with three mileposts, and this research reads it that way too. But Daniel attaches events to each marker that didn't happen in AD 30. God fixed Passover to Pentecost at fifty days: "even unto the morrow after the seventh sabbath shall ye number fifty days" (Leviticus 23:16), not forty-five. The 1,260 ends "when he shall have accomplished to scatter the power of the holy people" (Daniel 12:7); every time Scripture uses this span, God's people are under an enemy's hand (Daniel 7:25; Revelation 12:14; 13:5-7).
The 1,290 begins with "the abomination that maketh desolate set up" (12:11), yet two days before that Passover Jesus still said, "When ye therefore shall see the abomination of desolation" (Matthew 24:15; 26:2). And Daniel 12:2's awakening from "the dust of the earth" hadn't happened by Pentecost: Peter said, "David... is both dead and buried, and his sepulchre is with us unto this day" (Acts 2:29). Daniel himself rests first, then stands "at the end of the days" (12:13).

O10. "The fulness of the Gentiles already came; Paul said 'ye are complete in him' (Colossians 2:10)."
Keywords: fulness of the Gentiles, Colossians 2:10, Romans 11:25, complete in him
Answer: Every believer is complete in Christ: "in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily. And ye are complete in him" (Colossians 2:9-10). But Romans 11:25 is about a number coming in over time: "blindness in part is happened to Israel, until the fulness of the Gentiles be come in." Sharing the word "fulness" doesn't make them one event.
Paul, writing in the same period, said the work wasn't finished: "Till we all come in the unity of the faith... unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ" (Ephesians 4:13). Near the end of his life he was still preaching "that all the Gentiles might hear" (2 Timothy 4:17). And if the fulness had come, Israel's blindness would have ended then, "And so all Israel shall be saved" (Romans 11:26). Gentiles are still coming in today: "this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and then shall the end come" (Matthew 24:14).

O11. "Isaiah 65's new heavens and earth still have death in them, so the new earth is now."

Answer: Isaiah says "the sinner being an hundred years old shall be accursed" (Isaiah 65:20). John answers it: "And there shall be no more curse" (Revelation 22:3), and "there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying" (21:4). Isaiah himself saw beyond 65:20: "He will swallow up death in victory" (Isaiah 25:8), and Paul says when: "when this corruptible shall have put on incorruption, and this mortal shall have put on immortality, then shall be brought to pass the saying that is written, Death is swallowed up in victory" (1 Corinthians 15:54).
This research reads the mortal details of Isaiah 65:20 as part of a promise God made to the nation conditionally, by His own rule: "at what instant I shall speak concerning a nation... to build and to plant it; If it do evil in my sight... then I will repent of the good" (Jeremiah 18:9-10). Peter and John carry the promise forward in its fullness, where death is gone.

O12. "Jesus told Caiaphas 'ye shall see,' so the coming on the clouds was the fall of Jerusalem."
Keywords: Caiaphas, Matthew 26:64, hereafter, cloud coming, Isaiah 19:1
Answer: From the trial on, Jesus sat "on the right hand of power" (Matthew 26:64), and Stephen saw it: "I see the heavens opened, and the Son of man standing on the right hand of God" (Acts 7:56). But the coming itself is defined by sight. "While they beheld, he was taken up; and a cloud received him out of their sight" (Acts 1:9), and He "shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go into heaven" (1:11). They watched a body rise until the cloud hid Him; a coming no one saw isn't "in like manner." Caiaphas will see Him, because "every eye shall see him, and they also which pierced him" (Revelation 1:7), at the resurrection "both of the just and unjust" (Acts 24:15).

O13. "The thousand years is symbolic and already past; Satan was bound at the cross."

They refused the mark before their reign began, so the reign comes after the beast's time, not before it. Satan is bound "that he should deceive the nations no more, till the thousand years should be fulfilled" (20:3). Yet the apostles wrote, "Satan hindered us" (1 Thessalonians 2:18) and "your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour" (1 Peter 5:8). A bound Satan who deceives no one isn't a roaring lion walking about. And the beast and false prophet are already in the lake when the devil joins them after the thousand years (20:10).

O14. "The 'little season' and Gog and Magog already happened in the first century."

Gog and Magog, to gather them together to battle" (Revelation 20:7-8). Objections from the pre-tribulation and mid-tribulation views

O15. "God has not appointed us to wrath, so the church must be removed before the tribulation."
Keywords: not appointed to wrath, 1 Thessalonians 5:9, removed before, wrath of God
Answer: Amen, believers are not appointed to God's wrath: "God hath not appointed us to wrath, but to obtain salvation by our Lord Jesus Christ" (1 Thessalonians 5:9). The question is whether God protects His people by removing them or by keeping them. Scripture shows two different things in Revelation. Persecution comes from the beast and the dragon: "it was given unto him to make war with the saints, and to overcome them" (Revelation 13:7), and "the devil is come down unto you, having great wrath" (12:12). God's wrath falls only on the unbelieving: the first bowl strikes "the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image" (16:2).
That is God's pattern from Egypt: "I will put a division between my people and thy people" (Exodus 8:23). Jesus prayed exactly this: "I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil" (John 17:15). So we are kept from the wrath, not spared all persecution: "These are they which came out of great tribulation" (Revelation 7:14).
Papers: pre-tribulation.html, the-shepherds-gathering.html

O16. "Luke 21:36 promises we will escape all these things."
Keywords: escape, accounted worthy, Luke 21:36, stand before the Son of man
Answer: It's a real promise, and Luke tells us in the same breath what we escape: "lest at any time your hearts be overcharged... and so that day come upon you unawares. For as a snare shall it come on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth. Watch ye therefore, and pray always, that ye may be accounted worthy to escape all these things" (Luke 21:34-36). The escape is from the snare of that day, the same day Paul describes: "sudden destruction cometh upon them... and they shall not escape. But ye, brethren, are not in darkness" (1 Thessalonians 5:3-4).
"All these things" in that chapter include persecution, and Jesus said some would die: "some of you shall they cause to be put to death... But there shall not an hair of your head perish" (Luke 21:16-18). So escape can't mean removal before trouble. To "stand before the Son of man" means being approved when He judges: "the ungodly shall not stand in the judgment" (Psalm 1:5). And Paul says believers are "counted worthy of the kingdom of God" through "persecutions and tribulations that ye endure" (2 Thessalonians 1:4-5).
Papers: pre-tribulation.html

O17. "Revelation 4:1, 'Come up hither,' is the rapture of the church."
Keywords: come up hither, Revelation 4:1, door opened in heaven, church in heaven
Answer: The words were spoken to John alone, so he could see the vision: "Come up hither, and I will shew thee things which must be hereafter" (Revelation 4:1). Later John is back on earth and told, "Rise, and measure the temple of God" (11:1). The only other "Come up hither" in Revelation comes after the two witnesses finish their 1,260 days, "and their enemies beheld them" (11:12), in full view of the world, not secretly.
Papers: pre-tribulation.html

O18. "The church isn't mentioned after Revelation 3, so it must be in heaven."
Keywords: church absent, no church in Revelation 4-19, saints are Israel
Answer: The word "church" isn't used, but God's people are everywhere in those chapters, under Scripture's other names for them: "the saints" (Revelation 13:7, 10; 14:12; 16:6; 17:6), "which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ" (Revelation 12:17), and "the faith of Jesus" (14:12). Jesus speaks to them during the sixth bowl: "Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments" (16:15). And at the end He says, "I Jesus have sent mine angel to testify unto you these things in the churches" (22:16): the whole book was written for the churches.

O19. "The restrainer of 2 Thessalonians 2 is the church, removed before the man of sin appears."
Keywords: restrainer, he who now letteth, taken out of the way, 2 Thessalonians 2:7
Answer: Paul calls the restrainer "he": "only he who now letteth will let, until he be taken out of the way" (2 Thessalonians 2:7). Scripture calls the church "she," the bride (Revelation 19:7-8). This research reads the restrainer as most likely the Holy Spirit, who steps aside from holding back Satan's plans when mercy closes, as before the flood: "My spirit shall not always strive with man" (Genesis 6:3). He doesn't leave the earth; a great multitude still comes to Christ "out of great tribulation" (Revelation 7:14), and no one comes without the Spirit (John 6:44). And Paul's order puts the gathering after the man of sin: "our gathering together unto him... that day shall not come, except there come a falling away first, and that man of sin be revealed" (2 Thessalonians 2:1, 3).

O20. "Daniel's seventieth week is a future seven-year tribulation, after a gap."
Keywords: 70th week, seven years, gap, Daniel 9:27, covenant with many, Antichrist treaty
Answer: Daniel's seventy weeks are one unbroken count: "Seventy weeks are determined upon thy people" (Daniel 9:24). They run from the decree of 457 BC to AD 34 (ten Jubilee cycles; see "How the dates are determined"). The "he" who confirms the covenant is the Messiah of the verse before: "after threescore and two weeks shall Messiah be cut off, but not for himself" (9:26), "And he shall confirm the covenant with many for one week: and in the midst of the week he shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease" (9:27). Jesus used the same words for His own covenant: "this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many" (Matthew 26:28). At the cross, in the midst of the week (AD 30), the veil tore and the sacrifices lost their purpose (Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:18). Nothing in the text lifts the last week out and moves it two thousand years.

O21. "There are two comings: a secret rapture for the church, then a visible return seven years later."
Keywords: two comings, secret rapture, signless, Noah and Lot, mid-trib, imminent
Answer: Paul gives only two stages of the resurrection: "Christ the firstfruits; afterward they that are Christ's at his coming" (1 Corinthians 15:23). The change happens "at the last trump" (15:52), and Revelation's seventh trumpet is the last (Revelation 11:15). Jesus' order is "Gather ye together first the tares... but gather the wheat into my barn" (Matthew 13:30): the wicked are not left behind for years while the wheat is removed. And His examples put rescue and judgment on the same day: "the same day that Lot went out of Sodom it rained fire and brimstone from heaven, and destroyed them all. Even thus shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed" (Luke 17:29-30).
The coming isn't quiet either: "the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God" (1 Thessalonians 4:16), "with a great sound of a trumpet" (Matthew 24:31), "Immediately after the tribulation of those days" (24:29). Two gatherings would also mean two classes of saints, but "there is no respect of persons with God" (Romans 2:11).

ID: 01
SECTION: The Timing and Signs
QUESTION: Can we know the day or the hour?
ALSO ASKED: Doesn't Jesus say no one knows the day or the hour? Is it wrong to count days to His return?
STATUS: approved

QUICK ANSWER
No one can set the date ahead of time: "But of that day and hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels of heaven, but my Father only" (Matthew 24:36). But in the very next verse Jesus said, "as the days of Noe were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" (24:37), and God told Noah the count once it began: "For yet seven days, and I will cause it to rain upon the earth" (Genesis 7:4). So the world is caught unaware, while those who watch can know it is near once the signs begin: "when ye shall see all these things, know that it is near, even at the doors" (24:33). Daniel's count of 1,260, 1,290 and 1,335 days can't be dated in advance; it can only be followed as it unfolds.

FULL STUDY
1. What Jesus said, and what He said next.
"But of that day and hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels of heaven, but my Father only. But as the days of Noe were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be. For as in the days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark, And knew not until the flood came, and took them all away; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" (Matthew 24:36-39). The ones who "knew not" were the world outside the ark.

2. Noah was told when the count began.
"And the LORD said unto Noah, Come thou and all thy house into the ark... For yet seven days, and I will cause it to rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights" (Genesis 7:1, 4). "And it came to pass after seven days, that the waters of the flood were upon the earth" (7:10). No one knew the day years before; once God started the count, Noah knew.

3. The watchers are not in darkness.
"But of the times and the seasons, brethren, ye have no need that I write unto you. For yourselves know perfectly that the day of the Lord so cometh as a thief in the night... But ye, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief" (1 Thessalonians 5:1-2, 4). The thief comes on those who aren't watching: "If therefore thou shalt not watch, I will come on thee as a thief, and thou shalt not know what hour I will come upon thee" (Revelation 3:3).

4. The fig tree.
"Now learn a parable of the fig tree; When his branch is yet tender, and putteth forth leaves, ye know that summer is nigh: So likewise ye, when ye shall see all these things, know that it is near, even at the doors" (Matthew 24:32-33).

5. God reveals before He acts.
"Surely the Lord GOD will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets" (Amos 3:7). Daniel's numbers were given to be counted by those living through them: "Blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days" (Daniel 12:12).

6. So: watch, don't date-set.
"Watch therefore: for ye know not what hour your Lord doth come" (Matthew 24:42). The count begins with the fourth seal (see entry on the signs and the timeline); until then, the call is readiness, not a date.

MY READING
Daniel's 1,260, 1,290 and 1,335 days are literal days, counted from the opening of the fourth seal, when the censer is cast down (Revelation 8:5). This framework publishes no future dates.

OBJECTIONS
- "Acts 1:7 says it isn't for us to know." Jesus said to the apostles, who asked whether He would "at this time restore again the kingdom to Israel" (Acts 1:6), "It is not for you to know the times or the seasons, which the Father hath put in his own power" (1:7). It answered their question about that time; it didn't cancel Daniel's count. The Father who holds the times is the One who "changeth the times and the seasons" and "revealeth the deep and secret things" (Daniel 2:21-22).
- "Mark 13:32 says not even the Son knew." "But of that day and that hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels which are in heaven, neither the Son, but the Father" (Mark 13:32). He spoke this while He "made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant" (Philippians 2:7). After His resurrection He gave John "The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him, to shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass" (Revelation 1:1).

ID: 02
SECTION: The Timing and Signs
QUESTION: What are the signs of the Second Coming?
ALSO ASKED: Are wars, earthquakes and disasters signs of the end? What happens before Jesus returns?
STATUS: approved

QUICK ANSWER
Jesus gave two kinds of signs. First, the general ones: "ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars... there shall be famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in divers places. All these are the beginning of sorrows" (Matthew 24:6-8), and He added, "the end is not yet" (24:6). Second, the signposts of the great tribulation itself, in Scripture's own order: a falling away and the man of sin revealed (2 Thessalonians 2:3), the great tribulation (Matthew 24:21), the abomination of Daniel 12:11, and then "Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven" (24:29), followed by "the sign of the Son of man in heaven" and the gathering (24:30-31). Today's world events can echo the "beginning of sorrows," but they don't start the count.

FULL STUDY
1. The beginning of sorrows: not yet the end.
"And ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars: see that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to pass, but the end is not yet. For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in divers places. All these are the beginning of sorrows" (Matthew 24:6-8). The first sign Jesus named was deception: "Take heed that no man deceive you. For many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ; and shall deceive many" (24:4-5).

2. The gospel goes to the world.
"And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and then shall the end come" (24:14).

3. The falling away and the man of sin.
"Let no man deceive you by any means: for that day shall not come, except there come a falling away first, and that man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition; Who opposeth and exalteth himself above all that is called God, or that is worshipped; so that he as God sitteth in the temple of God, shewing himself that he is God" (2 Thessalonians 2:3-4).

4. The great tribulation.
"For then shall be great tribulation, such as was not since the beginning of the world to this time, no, nor ever shall be. And except those days should be shortened, there should no flesh be saved: but for the elect's sake those days shall be shortened" (Matthew 24:21-22). Daniel uses the same words: "there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time: and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book" (Daniel 12:1).

5. Daniel's mileposts.
"From the time that the daily sacrifice shall be taken away, and the abomination that maketh desolate set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days. Blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days" (Daniel 12:11-12).

6. The signs in the sky, and the coming.
"Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken: And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven: and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn, and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory. And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other" (Matthew 24:29-31). John saw the same at the sixth seal: "the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the moon became as blood; And the stars of heaven fell unto the earth" (Revelation 6:12-13).

MY READING
The great tribulation's signposts in order (see the timeline): day 1, the fourth seal opens and the censer is cast down after the 144,000 are sealed (Revelation 7:1-3; 8:5); about day 1,260, the two witnesses finish their testimony, mercy closes, and the seven last plagues begin (Revelation 11:7; 15:1, 8; 22:11); day 1,290, the abomination of Daniel 12:11, a death decree against all who refuse the image of the beast (Revelation 13:15); about day 1,335, the last trump, the sixth-seal signs, and Christ's appearing. The day numbers are this framework's reading; Scripture gives the order.

OBJECTIONS
- "Today's disasters prove we're in the great tribulation." Jesus called wars, famines and earthquakes "the beginning of sorrows" and said "the end is not yet" (Matthew 24:6, 8). The great tribulation's own signposts, such as the man of sin revealed (2 Thessalonians 2:3-4), haven't appeared.
- "The church won't see any of this." Paul told the church that the gathering comes after the falling away and the man of sin: "by our gathering together unto him... that day shall not come, except there come a falling away first" (2 Thessalonians 2:1, 3). See entry 07.

ID: 03
SECTION: The Timing and Signs
QUESTION: Are we living in the end times right now?
ALSO ASKED: Is this the last generation? Has the tribulation started?
STATUS: approved

QUICK ANSWER
Yes and not yet. Scripture's "last days" began at Pentecost: Peter said, "this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel; And it shall come to pass in the last days, saith God, I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh" (Acts 2:16-17). But Daniel's "time of the end" (Daniel 12:4, 9), the great tribulation, is a distinct final era, and its signposts haven't appeared. So we live in the last days, and the call is to be ready: "Watch ye therefore: for ye know not when the master of the house cometh" (Mark 13:35).

FULL STUDY
1. The last days began with Christ.
"God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son" (Hebrews 1:1-2). "Little children, it is the last time" (1 John 2:18). Peter at Pentecost: "this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel; And it shall come to pass in the last days" (Acts 2:16-17).

2. The last days have a character.
"This know also, that in the last days perilous times shall come. For men shall be lovers of their own selves, covetous, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy... Having a form of godliness, but denying the power thereof" (2 Timothy 3:1-2, 5).

3. "The time of the end" is the final era.
Daniel was told, "shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end" (Daniel 12:4), and "the words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end" (12:9). Daniel 11 marks the turn: the persecution of the faithful runs "even to the time of the end: because it is yet for a time appointed" (11:35), and "at the time of the end" the final king comes (11:40).

4. What to do now.
"Take ye heed, watch and pray: for ye know not when the time is" (Mark 13:33). "And what I say unto you I say unto all, Watch" (13:37). The wise virgins had oil enough for a long night: "the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps. While the bridegroom tarried, they all slumbered and slept" (Matthew 25:4-5).

MY READING
The first three seals of Revelation 6 have already been opened; the great tribulation begins when the fourth seal opens. In this research the seventieth Jubilee, completed in 1994, closed the long era when prophetic time was counted a day for a year; the prophecies still ahead are counted in literal days. Neither point sets a date. Credit: the Jubilee rule for prophetic time comes from Larry Wilson's research.

OBJECTIONS
- "The signs are all around us, so the tribulation has begun." The great tribulation's signposts are specific: the falling away and the man of sin revealed (2 Thessalonians 2:3-4), the abomination of Daniel 12:11, and the sixth-seal signs (Matthew 24:29). General troubles are "the beginning of sorrows" (24:8). See entry 02.
- "Every generation thought it was the last." Every generation was told to watch (Mark 13:37), and the dead in Christ lose nothing by waiting: "the dead in Christ shall rise first" (1 Thessalonians 4:16).

ID: 04
SECTION: The Timing and Signs
QUESTION: What is "the generation that will not pass away"?
ALSO ASKED: Did Matthew 24:34 mean everything happened by AD 70? Was Jesus wrong about "this generation"?
STATUS: approved

QUICK ANSWER
Jesus used "this generation" twice that week, for two events. Over Jerusalem He said, "All these things shall come upon this generation... Behold, your house is left unto you desolate" (Matthew 23:36, 38). That came forty years after the cross, in AD 70; Scripture's generation under judgment is forty years (Numbers 14:33-34; Psalm 95:10). On the Mount of Olives, after the sky signs and His coming, He said, "So likewise ye, when ye shall see all these things, know that it is near, even at the doors. Verily I say unto you, This generation shall not pass, till all these things be fulfilled" (24:33-34). That is the generation that sees "all these things": the signs in the sun, moon and stars, and the Son of man coming in the clouds (24:29-30).

FULL STUDY
1. The judgment on Jerusalem: forty years.
"Fill ye up then the measure of your fathers... All these things shall come upon this generation. O Jerusalem, Jerusalem... Behold, your house is left unto you desolate" (Matthew 23:32, 36-38). "Forty years long was I grieved with this generation" (Psalm 95:10); "your children shall wander in the wilderness forty years" (Numbers 14:33). From the cross in AD 30 to the Temple's fall in AD 70 is forty years.

2. The sign Jesus attached to Matthew 24:34.
The verse comes right after the fig tree: "when ye shall see all these things, know that it is near" (24:33). "All these things" include "Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened... and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory. And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect" (24:29-31).

3. Luke shows the span between them.
Luke records the fall: "they shall fall by the edge of the sword, and shall be led away captive into all nations: and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of the Gentiles, until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled" (Luke 21:24). Only after that span: "And there shall be signs in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars... And then shall they see the Son of man coming in a cloud with power and great glory" (21:25, 27). And it comes "as a snare... on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth" (21:35).

4. What did not happen in AD 70.
"For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in Christ shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds" (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17). "Behold, he cometh with clouds; and every eye shall see him" (Revelation 1:7). Paul warned of men "saying that the resurrection is past already; and overthrow the faith of some" (2 Timothy 2:18).

MY READING
Matthew 23:36's "this generation" is the forty years from the cross (AD 30) to the Temple's fall (AD 70). Matthew 24:34's "this generation" is the generation that sees the sixth-seal sky signs that mark His imminent return.

OBJECTIONS
- "'This generation' always means the people listening." Jesus tied 24:34 to a condition, "when ye shall see all these things" (24:33), and "all these things" include the sky signs and the gathering of the elect (24:29-31), which did not happen in AD 70. Luke places "the times of the Gentiles" between the fall of Jerusalem and the signs (Luke 21:24-25).
- "The coming in AD 70 was spiritual." Jesus warned against a coming no one could see: "if they shall say unto you, Behold, he is in the desert; go not forth: behold, he is in the secret chambers; believe it not. For as the lightning cometh out of the east, and shineth even unto the west; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be" (Matthew 24:26-27). The angels said He would come "in like manner as ye have seen him go into heaven" (Acts 1:11).

1:3 Blessed is he that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy, and keep those things which are written therein: for the time is at hand.
1:4 John to the seven churches which are in Asia: Grace be unto you, and peace, from him which is, and which was, and which is to come; and from the seven Spirits which are before his throne;
1:5 And from Jesus Christ, who is the faithful witness, and the first begotten of the dead, and the prince of the kings of the earth. Unto him that loved us, and washed us from our sins in his own blood,
1:6 And hath made us kings and priests unto God and his Father; to him be glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen.
1:7 Behold, he cometh with clouds; and every eye shall see him, and they also which pierced him: and all kindreds of the earth shall wail because of him. Even so, Amen.
1:8 I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the ending, saith the Lord, which is, and which was, and which is to come, the Almighty.
1:9 I John, who also am your brother, and companion in tribulation, and in the kingdom and patience of Jesus Christ, was in the isle that is called Patmos, for the word of God, and for the testimony of Jesus Christ.
1:10 I was in the Spirit on the Lord’s day, and heard behind me a great voice, as of a trumpet,
1:11 Saying, I am Alpha and Omega, the first and the last: and, What thou seest, write in a book, and send it unto the seven churches which are in Asia; unto Ephesus, and unto Smyrna, and unto Pergamos, and unto Thyatira, and unto Sardis, and unto Philadelphia, and unto Laodicea.
1:12 And I turned to see the voice that spake with me. And being turned, I saw seven golden candlesticks;
1:13 And in the midst of the seven candlesticks one like unto the Son of man, clothed with a garment down to the foot, and girt about the paps with a golden girdle.
1:14 His head and his hairs were white like wool, as white as snow; and his eyes were as a flame of fire;
1:15 And his feet like unto fine brass, as if they burned in a furnace; and his voice as the sound of many waters.
1:16 And he had in his right hand seven stars: and out of his mouth went a sharp twoedged sword: and his countenance was as the sun shineth in his strength.
1:17 And when I saw him, I fell at his feet as dead. And he laid his right hand upon me, saying unto me, Fear not; I am the first and the last:
1:18 I am he that liveth, and was dead; and, behold, I am alive for evermore, Amen; and have the keys of hell and of death.
1:19 Write the things which thou hast seen, and the things which are, and the things which shall be hereafter;
1:20 The mystery of the seven stars which thou sawest in my right hand, and the seven golden candlesticks. The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches: and the seven candlesticks which thou sawest are the seven churches.

Revelation 2
2:1 Unto the angel of the church of Ephesus write; These things saith he that holdeth the seven stars in his right hand, who walketh in the midst of the seven golden candlesticks;
2:2 I know thy works, and thy labour, and thy patience, and how thou canst not bear them which are evil: and thou hast tried them which say they are apostles, and are not, and hast found them liars:
2:3 And hast borne, and hast patience, and for my name’s sake hast laboured, and hast not fainted.
2:4 Nevertheless I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love.
2:5 Remember therefore from whence thou art fallen, and repent, and do the first works; or else I will come unto thee quickly, and will remove thy candlestick out of his place, except thou repent.
2:6 But this thou hast, that thou hatest the deeds of the Nicolaitanes, which I also hate.
2:7 He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; To him that overcometh will I give to eat of the tree of life, which is in the midst of the paradise of God.
2:8 And unto the angel of the church in Smyrna write; These things saith the first and the last, which was dead, and is alive;
2:9 I know thy works, and tribulation, and poverty, (but thou art rich) and I know the blasphemy of them which say they are Jews, and are not, but are the synagogue of Satan.
2:10 Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer: behold, the devil shall cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tried; and ye shall have tribulation ten days: be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee a crown of life.
2:11 He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; He that overcometh shall not be hurt of the second death.
2:12 And to the angel of the church in Pergamos write; These things saith he which hath the sharp sword with two edges;
2:13 I know thy works, and where thou dwellest, even where Satan’s seat is: and thou holdest fast my name, and hast not denied my faith, even in those days wherein Antipas was my faithful martyr, who was slain among you, where Satan dwelleth.
2:14 But I have a few things against thee, because thou hast there them that hold the doctrine of Balaam, who taught Balac to cast a stumblingblock before the children of Israel, to eat things sacrificed unto idols, and to commit fornication.
2:15 So hast thou also them that hold the doctrine of the Nicolaitanes, which thing I hate.
2:16 Repent; or else I will come unto thee quickly, and will fight against them with the sword of my mouth.
2:17 He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; To him that overcometh will I give to eat of the hidden manna, and will give him a white stone, and in the stone a new name written, which no man knoweth saving he that receiveth it.
2:18 And unto the angel of the church in Thyatira write; These things saith the Son of God, who hath his eyes like unto a flame of fire, and his feet are like fine brass;
2:19 I know thy works, and charity, and service, and faith, and thy patience, and thy works; and the last to be more than the first.
2:20 Notwithstanding I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication, and to eat things sacrificed unto idols.
2:21 And I gave her space to repent of her fornication; and she repented not.
2:22 Behold, I will cast her into a bed, and them that commit adultery with her into great tribulation, except they repent of their deeds.
2:23 And I will kill her children with death; and all the churches shall know that I am he which searcheth the reins and hearts: and I will give unto every one of you according to your works.
2:24 But unto you I say, and unto the rest in Thyatira, as many as have not this doctrine, and which have not known the depths of Satan, as they speak; I will put upon you none other burden.
2:25 But that which ye have already hold fast till I come.
2:26 And he that overcometh, and keepeth my works unto the end, to him will I give power over the nations:
2:27 And he shall rule them with a rod of iron; as the vessels of a potter shall they be broken to shivers: even as I received of my Father.
2:28 And I will give him the morning star.
2:29 He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches.

Revelation 3
3:1 And unto the angel of the church in Sardis write; These things saith he that hath the seven Spirits of God, and the seven stars; I know thy works, that thou hast a name that thou livest, and art dead.
3:2 Be watchful, and strengthen the things which remain, that are ready to die: for I have not found thy works perfect before God.
3:3 Remember therefore how thou hast received and heard, and hold fast, and repent. If therefore thou shalt not watch, I will come on thee as a thief, and thou shalt not know what hour I will come upon thee.
3:4 Thou hast a few names even in Sardis which have not defiled their garments; and they shall walk with me in white: for they are worthy.
3:5 He that overcometh, the same shall be clothed in white raiment; and I will not blot out his name out of the book of life, but I will confess his name before my Father, and before his angels.
3:6 He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches.
3:7 And to the angel of the church in Philadelphia write; These things saith he that is holy, he that is true, he that hath the key of David, he that openeth, and no man shutteth; and shutteth, and no man openeth;
3:8 I know thy works: behold, I have set before thee an open door, and no man can shut it: for thou hast a little strength, and hast kept my word, and hast not denied my name.
3:9 Behold, I will make them of the synagogue of Satan, which say they are Jews, and are not, but do lie; behold, I will make them to come and worship before thy feet, and to know that I have loved thee.
3:10 Because thou hast kept the word of my patience, I also will keep thee from the hour of temptation, which shall come upon all the world, to try them that dwell upon the earth.
3:11 Behold, I come quickly: hold that fast which thou hast, that no man take thy crown.
3:12 Him that overcometh will I make a pillar in the temple of my God, and he shall go no more out: and I will write upon him the name of my God, and the name of the city of my God, which is new Jerusalem, which cometh down out of heaven from my God: and I will write upon him my new name.
3:13 He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches.
3:14 And unto the angel of the church of the Laodiceans write; These things saith the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the beginning of the creation of God;
3:15 I know thy works, that thou art neither cold nor hot: I would thou wert cold or hot.
3:16 So then because thou art lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I will spue thee out of my mouth.
3:17 Because thou sayest, I am rich, and increased with goods, and have need of nothing; and knowest not that thou art wretched, and miserable, and poor, and blind, and naked:
3:18 I counsel thee to buy of me gold tried in the fire, that thou mayest be rich; and white raiment, that thou mayest be clothed, and that the shame of thy nakedness do not appear; and anoint thine eyes with eyesalve, that thou mayest see.
3:19 As many as I love, I rebuke and chasten: be zealous therefore, and repent.
3:20 Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me.
3:21 To him that overcometh will I grant to sit with me in my throne, even as I also overcame, and am set down with my Father in his throne.
3:22 He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches.

Revelation 4
4:1 After this I looked, and, behold, a door was opened in heaven: and the first voice which I heard was as it were of a trumpet talking with me; which said, Come up hither, and I will shew thee things which must be hereafter.
4:2 And immediately I was in the spirit: and, behold, a throne was set in heaven, and one sat on the throne.
4:3 And he that sat was to look upon like a jasper and a sardine stone: and there was a rainbow round about the throne, in sight like unto an emerald.
4:4 And round about the throne were four and twenty seats: and upon the seats I saw four and twenty elders sitting, clothed in white raiment; and they had on their heads crowns of gold.
4:5 And out of the throne proceeded lightnings and thunderings and voices: and there were seven lamps of fire burning before the throne, which are the seven Spirits of God.
4:6 And before the throne there was a sea of glass like unto crystal: and in the midst of the throne, and round about the throne, were four beasts full of eyes before and behind.
4:7 And the first beast was like a lion, and the second beast like a calf, and the third beast had a face as a man, and the fourth beast was like a flying eagle.
4:8 And the four beasts had each of them six wings about him; and they were full of eyes within: and they rest not day and night, saying, Holy, holy, holy, LORD God Almighty, which was, and is, and is to come.
4:9 And when those beasts give glory and honour and thanks to him that sat on the throne, who liveth for ever and ever,
4:10 The four and twenty elders fall down before him that sat on the throne, and worship him that liveth for ever and ever, and cast their crowns before the throne, saying,
4:11 Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honour and power: for thou hast created all things, and for thy pleasure they are and were created.

Revelation 5
5:1 And I saw in the right hand of him that sat on the throne a book written within and on the backside, sealed with seven seals.
5:2 And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice, Who is worthy to open the book, and to loose the seals thereof?
5:3 And no man in heaven, nor in earth, neither under the earth, was able to open the book, neither to look thereon.
5:4 And I wept much, because no man was found worthy to open and to read the book, neither to look thereon.
5:5 And one of the elders saith unto me, Weep not: behold, the Lion of the tribe of Juda, the Root of David, hath prevailed to open the book, and to loose the seven seals thereof.
5:6 And I beheld, and, lo, in the midst of the throne and of the four beasts, and in the midst of the elders, stood a Lamb as it had been slain, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God sent forth into all the earth.
5:7 And he came and took the book out of the right hand of him that sat upon the throne.
5:8 And when he had taken the book, the four beasts and four and twenty elders fell down before the Lamb, having every one of them harps, and golden vials full of odours, which are the prayers of saints.
5:9 And they sung a new song, saying, Thou art worthy to take the book, and to open the seals thereof: for thou wast slain, and hast redeemed us to God by thy blood out of every kindred, and tongue, and people, and nation;
5:10 And hast made us unto our God kings and priests: and we shall reign on the earth.
5:11 And I beheld, and I heard the voice of many angels round about the throne and the beasts and the elders: and the number of them was ten thousand times ten thousand, and thousands of thousands;
5:12 Saying with a loud voice, Worthy is the Lamb that was slain to receive power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing.
5:13 And every creature which is in heaven, and on the earth, and under the earth, and such as are in the sea, and all that are in them, heard I saying, Blessing, and honour, and glory, and power, be unto him that sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb for ever and ever.
5:14 And the four beasts said, Amen. And the four and twenty elders fell down and worshipped him that liveth for ever and ever.

Revelation 6
6:1 And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seals, and I heard, as it were the noise of thunder, one of the four beasts saying, Come and see.
6:2 And I saw, and behold a white horse: and he that sat on him had a bow; and a crown was given unto him: and he went forth conquering, and to conquer.
6:3 And when he had opened the second seal, I heard the second beast say, Come and see.
6:4 And there went out another horse that was red: and power was given to him that sat thereon to take peace from the earth, and that they should kill one another: and there was given unto him a great sword.
6:5 And when he had opened the third seal, I heard the third beast say, Come and see. And I beheld, and lo a black horse; and he that sat on him had a pair of balances in his hand.
6:6 And I heard a voice in the midst of the four beasts say, A measure of wheat for a penny, and three measures of barley for a penny; and see thou hurt not the oil and the wine.
6:7 And when he had opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth beast say, Come and see.
6:8 And I looked, and behold a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him. And power was given unto them over the fourth part of the earth, to kill with sword, and with hunger, and with death, and with the beasts of the earth.
6:9 And when he had opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of them that were slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held:
6:10 And they cried with a loud voice, saying, How long, O Lord, holy and true, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth?
6:11 And white robes were given unto every one of them; and it was said unto them, that they should rest yet for a little season, until their fellowservants also and their brethren, that should be killed as they were, should be fulfilled.
6:12 And I beheld when he had opened the sixth seal, and, lo, there was a great earthquake; and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the moon became as blood;
6:13 And the stars of heaven fell unto the earth, even as a fig tree casteth her untimely figs, when she is shaken of a mighty wind.
6:14 And the heaven departed as a scroll when it is rolled together; and every mountain and island were moved out of their places.
6:15 And the kings of the earth, and the great men, and the rich men, and the chief captains, and the mighty men, and every bondman, and every free man, hid themselves in the dens and in the rocks of the mountains;
6:16 And said to the mountains and rocks, Fall on us, and hide us from the face of him that sitteth on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb:
6:17 For the great day of his wrath is come; and who shall be able to stand?

Revelation 7
7:1 And after these things I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, that the wind should not blow on the earth, nor on the sea, nor on any tree.
7:2 And I saw another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God: and he cried with a loud voice to the four angels, to whom it was given to hurt the earth and the sea,
7:3 Saying, Hurt not the earth, neither the sea, nor the trees, till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads.
7:4 And I heard the number of them which were sealed: and there were sealed an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel.
7:5 Of the tribe of Juda were sealed twelve thousand. Of the tribe of Reuben were sealed twelve thousand. Of the tribe of Gad were sealed twelve thousand.
7:6 Of the tribe of Aser were sealed twelve thousand. Of the tribe of Nephthalim were sealed twelve thousand. Of the tribe of Manasses were sealed twelve thousand.
7:7 Of the tribe of Simeon were sealed twelve thousand. Of the tribe of Levi were sealed twelve thousand. Of the tribe of Issachar were sealed twelve thousand.
7:8 Of the tribe of Zabulon were sealed twelve thousand. Of the tribe of Joseph were sealed twelve thousand. Of the tribe of Benjamin were sealed twelve thousand.
7:9 After this I beheld, and, lo, a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues, stood before the throne, and before the Lamb, clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands;
7:10 And cried with a loud voice, saying, Salvation to our God which sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb.
7:11 And all the angels stood round about the throne, and about the elders and the four beasts, and fell before the throne on their faces, and worshipped God,
7:12 Saying, Amen: Blessing, and glory, and wisdom, and thanksgiving, and honour, and power, and might, be unto our God for ever and ever. Amen.
7:13 And one of the elders answered, saying unto me, What are these which are arrayed in white robes? and whence came they?
7:14 And I said unto him, Sir, thou knowest. And he said to me, These are they which came out of great tribulation, and have washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb.
7:15 Therefore are they before the throne of God, and serve him day and night in his temple: and he that sitteth on the throne shall dwell among them.
7:16 They shall hunger no more, neither thirst any more; neither shall the sun light on them, nor any heat.
7:17 For the Lamb which is in the midst of the throne shall feed them, and shall lead them unto living fountains of waters: and God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes.

Revelation 8
8:1 And when he had opened the seventh seal, there was silence in heaven about the space of half an hour.
8:2 And I saw the seven angels which stood before God; and to them were given seven trumpets.
8:3 And another angel came and stood at the altar, having a golden censer; and there was given unto him much incense, that he should offer it with the prayers of all saints upon the golden altar which was before the throne.
8:4 And the smoke of the incense, which came with the prayers of the saints, ascended up before God out of the angel’s hand.
8:5 And the angel took the censer, and filled it with fire of the altar, and cast it into the earth: and there were voices, and thunderings, and lightnings, and an earthquake.
8:6 And the seven angels which had the seven trumpets prepared themselves to sound.
8:7 The first angel sounded, and there followed hail and fire mingled with blood, and they were cast upon the earth: and the third part of trees was burnt up, and all green grass was burnt up.
8:8 And the second angel sounded, and as it were a great mountain burning with fire was cast into the sea: and the third part of the sea became blood;
8:9 And the third part of the creatures which were in the sea, and had life, died; and the third part of the ships were destroyed.
8:10 And the third angel sounded, and there fell a great star from heaven, burning as it were a lamp, and it fell upon the third part of the rivers, and upon the fountains of waters;
8:11 And the name of the star is called Wormwood: and the third part of the waters became wormwood; and many men died of the waters, because they were made bitter.
8:12 And the fourth angel sounded, and the third part of the sun was smitten, and the third part of the moon, and the third part of the stars; so as the third part of them was darkened, and the day shone not for a third part of it, and the night likewise.
8:13 And I beheld, and heard an angel flying through the midst of heaven, saying with a loud voice, Woe, woe, woe, to the inhabiters of the earth by reason of the other voices of the trumpet of the three angels, which are yet to sound!

Revelation 9
9:1 And the fifth angel sounded, and I saw a star fall from heaven unto the earth: and to him was given the key of the bottomless pit.
9:2 And he opened the bottomless pit; and there arose a smoke out of the pit, as the smoke of a great furnace; and the sun and the air were darkened by reason of the smoke of the pit.
9:3 And there came out of the smoke locusts upon the earth: and unto them was given power, as the scorpions of the earth have power.
9:4 And it was commanded them that they should not hurt the grass of the earth, neither any green thing, neither any tree; but only those men which have not the seal of God in their foreheads.
9:5 And to them it was given that they should not kill them, but that they should be tormented five months: and their torment was as the torment of a scorpion, when he striketh a man.
9:6 And in those days shall men seek death, and shall not find it; and shall desire to die, and death shall flee from them.
9:7 And the shapes of the locusts were like unto horses prepared unto battle; and on their heads were as it were crowns like gold, and their faces were as the faces of men.
9:8 And they had hair as the hair of women, and their teeth were as the teeth of lions.
9:9 And they had breastplates, as it were breastplates of iron; and the sound of their wings was as the sound of chariots of many horses running to battle.
9:10 And they had tails like unto scorpions, and there were stings in their tails: and their power was to hurt men five months.
9:11 And they had a king over them, which is the angel of the bottomless pit, whose name in the Hebrew tongue is Abaddon, but in the Greek tongue hath his name Apollyon.
9:12 One woe is past; and, behold, there come two woes more hereafter.
9:13 And the sixth angel sounded, and I heard a voice from the four horns of the golden altar which is before God,
9:14 Saying to the sixth angel which had the trumpet, Loose the four angels which are bound in the great river Euphrates.
9:15 And the four angels were loosed, which were prepared for an hour, and a day, and a month, and a year, for to slay the third part of men.
9:16 And the number of the army of the horsemen were two hundred thousand thousand: and I heard the number of them.
9:17 And thus I saw the horses in the vision, and them that sat on them, having breastplates of fire, and of jacinth, and brimstone: and the heads of the horses were as the heads of lions; and out of their mouths issued fire and smoke and brimstone.
9:18 By these three was the third part of men killed, by the fire, and by the smoke, and by the brimstone, which issued out of their mouths.
9:19 For their power is in their mouth, and in their tails: for their tails were like unto serpents, and had heads, and with them they do hurt.
9:20 And the rest of the men which were not killed by these plagues yet repented not of the works of their hands, that they should not worship devils, and idols of gold, and silver, and brass, and stone, and of wood: which neither can see, nor hear, nor walk:
9:21 Neither repented they of their murders, nor of their sorceries, nor of their fornication, nor of their thefts.

Revelation 10
10:1 And I saw another mighty angel come down from heaven, clothed with a cloud: and a rainbow was upon his head, and his face was as it were the sun, and his feet as pillars of fire:
10:2 And he had in his hand a little book open: and he set his right foot upon the sea, and his left foot on the earth,
10:3 And cried with a loud voice, as when a lion roareth: and when he had cried, seven thunders uttered their voices.
10:4 And when the seven thunders had uttered their voices, I was about to write: and I heard a voice from heaven saying unto me, Seal up those things which the seven thunders uttered, and write them not.
10:5 And the angel which I saw stand upon the sea and upon the earth lifted up his hand to heaven,
10:6 And sware by him that liveth for ever and ever, who created heaven, and the things that therein are, and the earth, and the things that therein are, and the sea, and the things which are therein, that there should be time no longer:
10:7 But in the days of the voice of the seventh angel, when he shall begin to sound, the mystery of God should be finished, as he hath declared to his servants the prophets.
10:8 And the voice which I heard from heaven spake unto me again, and said, Go and take the little book which is open in the hand of the angel which standeth upon the sea and upon the earth.
10:9 And I went unto the angel, and said unto him, Give me the little book. And he said unto me, Take it, and eat it up; and it shall make thy belly bitter, but it shall be in thy mouth sweet as honey.
10:10 And I took the little book out of the angel’s hand, and ate it up; and it was in my mouth sweet as honey: and as soon as I had eaten it, my belly was bitter.
10:11 And he said unto me, Thou must prophesy again before many peoples, and nations, and tongues, and kings.

Revelation 11
11:1 And there was given me a reed like unto a rod: and the angel stood, saying, Rise, and measure the temple of God, and the altar, and them that worship therein.
11:2 But the court which is without the temple leave out, and measure it not; for it is given unto the Gentiles: and the holy city shall they tread under foot forty and two months.
11:3 And I will give power unto my two witnesses, and they shall prophesy a thousand two hundred and threescore days, clothed in sackcloth.
11:4 These are the two olive trees, and the two candlesticks standing before the God of the earth.
11:5 And if any man will hurt them, fire proceedeth out of their mouth, and devoureth their enemies: and if any man will hurt them, he must in this manner be killed.
11:6 These have power to shut heaven, that it rain not in the days of their prophecy: and have power over waters to turn them to blood, and to smite the earth with all plagues, as often as they will.
11:7 And when they shall have finished their testimony, the beast that ascendeth out of the bottomless pit shall make war against them, and shall overcome them, and kill them.
11:8 And their dead bodies shall lie in the street of the great city, which spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt, where also our Lord was crucified.
11:9 And they of the people and kindreds and tongues and nations shall see their dead bodies three days and an half, and shall not suffer their dead bodies to be put in graves.
11:10 And they that dwell upon the earth shall rejoice over them, and make merry, and shall send gifts one to another; because these two prophets tormented them that dwelt on the earth.
11:11 And after three days and an half the Spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet; and great fear fell upon them which saw them.
11:12 And they heard a great voice from heaven saying unto them, Come up hither. And they ascended up to heaven in a cloud; and their enemies beheld them.
11:13 And the same hour was there a great earthquake, and the tenth part of the city fell, and in the earthquake were slain of men seven thousand: and the remnant were affrighted, and gave glory to the God of heaven.
11:14 The second woe is past; and, behold, the third woe cometh quickly.
11:15 And the seventh angel sounded; and there were great voices in heaven, saying, The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ; and he shall reign for ever and ever.
11:16 And the four and twenty elders, which sat before God on their seats, fell upon their faces, and worshipped God,
11:17 Saying, We give thee thanks, O LORD God Almighty, which art, and wast, and art to come; because thou hast taken to thee thy great power, and hast reigned.
11:18 And the nations were angry, and thy wrath is come, and the time of the dead, that they should be judged, and that thou shouldest give reward unto thy servants the prophets, and to the saints, and them that fear thy name, small and great; and shouldest destroy them which destroy the earth.
11:19 And the temple of God was opened in heaven, and there was seen in his temple the ark of his testament: and there were lightnings, and voices, and thunderings, and an earthquake, and great hail.

Revelation 12
12:1 And there appeared a great wonder in heaven; a woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars:
12:2 And she being with child cried, travailing in birth, and pained to be delivered.
12:3 And there appeared another wonder in heaven; and behold a great red dragon, having seven heads and ten horns, and seven crowns upon his heads.
12:4 And his tail drew the third part of the stars of heaven, and did cast them to the earth: and the dragon stood before the woman which was ready to be delivered, for to devour her child as soon as it was born.
12:5 And she brought forth a man child, who was to rule all nations with a rod of iron: and her child was caught up unto God, and to his throne.
12:6 And the woman fled into the wilderness, where she hath a place prepared of God, that they should feed her there a thousand two hundred and threescore days.
12:7 And there was war in heaven: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon; and the dragon fought and his angels,
12:8 And prevailed not; neither was their place found any more in heaven.
12:9 And the great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world: he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him.
12:10 And I heard a loud voice saying in heaven, Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God, and the power of his Christ: for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night.
12:11 And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.
12:12 Therefore rejoice, ye heavens, and ye that dwell in them. Woe to the inhabiters of the earth and of the sea! for the devil is come down unto you, having great wrath, because he knoweth that he hath but a short time.
12:13 And when the dragon saw that he was cast unto the earth, he persecuted the woman which brought forth the man child.
12:14 And to the woman were given two wings of a great eagle, that she might fly into the wilderness, into her place, where she is nourished for a time, and times, and half a time, from the face of the serpent.
12:15 And the serpent cast out of his mouth water as a flood after the woman, that he might cause her to be carried away of the flood.
12:16 And the earth helped the woman, and the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed up the flood which the dragon cast out of his mouth.
12:17 And the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ.

Revelation 13
13:1 And I stood upon the sand of the sea, and saw a beast rise up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns, and upon his horns ten crowns, and upon his heads the name of blasphemy.
13:2 And the beast which I saw was like unto a leopard, and his feet were as the feet of a bear, and his mouth as the mouth of a lion: and the dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority.
13:3 And I saw one of his heads as it were wounded to death; and his deadly wound was healed: and all the world wondered after the beast.
13:4 And they worshipped the dragon which gave power unto the beast: and they worshipped the beast, saying, Who is like unto the beast? who is able to make war with him?
13:5 And there was given unto him a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies; and power was given unto him to continue forty and two months.
13:6 And he opened his mouth in blasphemy against God, to blaspheme his name, and his tabernacle, and them that dwell in heaven.
13:7 And it was given unto him to make war with the saints, and to overcome them: and power was given him over all kindreds, and tongues, and nations.
13:8 And all that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world.
13:9 If any man have an ear, let him hear.
13:10 He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints.
13:11 And I beheld another beast coming up out of the earth; and he had two horns like a lamb, and he spake as a dragon.
13:12 And he exerciseth all the power of the first beast before him, and causeth the earth and them which dwell therein to worship the first beast, whose deadly wound was healed.
13:13 And he doeth great wonders, so that he maketh fire come down from heaven on the earth in the sight of men,
13:14 And deceiveth them that dwell on the earth by the means of those miracles which he had power to do in the sight of the beast; saying to them that dwell on the earth, that they should make an image to the beast, which had the wound by a sword, and did live.
13:15 And he had power to give life unto the image of the beast, that the image of the beast should both speak, and cause that as many as would not worship the image of the beast should be killed.
13:16 And he causeth all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads:
13:17 And that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark, or the name of the beast, or the number of his name.
13:18 Here is wisdom. Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast: for it is the number of a man; and his number is Six hundred threescore and six.

Revelation 14
14:1 And I looked, and, lo, a Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father’s name written in their foreheads.
14:2 And I heard a voice from heaven, as the voice of many waters, and as the voice of a great thunder: and I heard the voice of harpers harping with their harps:
14:3 And they sung as it were a new song before the throne, and before the four beasts, and the elders: and no man could learn that song but the hundred and forty and four thousand, which were redeemed from the earth.
14:4 These are they which were not defiled with women; for they are virgins. These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth. These were redeemed from among men, being the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb.
14:5 And in their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault before the throne of God.
14:6 And I saw another angel fly in the midst of heaven, having the everlasting gospel to preach unto them that dwell on the earth, and to every nation, and kindred, and tongue, and people,
14:7 Saying with a loud voice, Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come: and worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters.
14:8 And there followed another angel, saying, Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication.
14:9 And the third angel followed them, saying with a loud voice, If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark in his forehead, or in his hand,
14:10 The same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation; and he shall be tormented with fire and brimstone in the presence of the holy angels, and in the presence of the Lamb:
14:11 And the smoke of their torment ascendeth up for ever and ever: and they have no rest day nor night, who worship the beast and his image, and whosoever receiveth the mark of his name.
14:12 Here is the patience of the saints: here are they that keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus.
14:13 And I heard a voice from heaven saying unto me, Write, Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth: Yea, saith the Spirit, that they may rest from their labours; and their works do follow them.
14:14 And I looked, and behold a white cloud, and upon the cloud one sat like unto the Son of man, having on his head a golden crown, and in his hand a sharp sickle.
14:15 And another angel came out of the temple, crying with a loud voice to him that sat on the cloud, Thrust in thy sickle, and reap: for the time is come for thee to reap; for the harvest of the earth is ripe.
14:16 And he that sat on the cloud thrust in his sickle on the earth; and the earth was reaped.
14:17 And another angel came out of the temple which is in heaven, he also having a sharp sickle.
14:18 And another angel came out from the altar, which had power over fire; and cried with a loud cry to him that had the sharp sickle, saying, Thrust in thy sharp sickle, and gather the clusters of the vine of the earth; for her grapes are fully ripe.
14:19 And the angel thrust in his sickle into the earth, and gathered the vine of the earth, and cast it into the great winepress of the wrath of God.
14:20 And the winepress was trodden without the city, and blood came out of the winepress, even unto the horse bridles, by the space of a thousand and six hundred furlongs.

Revelation 15
15:1 And I saw another sign in heaven, great and marvellous, seven angels having the seven last plagues; for in them is filled up the wrath of God.
15:2 And I saw as it were a sea of glass mingled with fire: and them that had gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark, and over the number of his name, stand on the sea of glass, having the harps of God.
15:3 And they sing the song of Moses the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying, Great and marvellous are thy works, Lord God Almighty; just and true are thy ways, thou King of saints.
15:4 Who shall not fear thee, O Lord, and glorify thy name? for thou only art holy: for all nations shall come and worship before thee; for thy judgments are made manifest.
15:5 And after that I looked, and, behold, the temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven was opened:
15:6 And the seven angels came out of the temple, having the seven plagues, clothed in pure and white linen, and having their breasts girded with golden girdles.
15:7 And one of the four beasts gave unto the seven angels seven golden vials full of the wrath of God, who liveth for ever and ever.
15:8 And the temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God, and from his power; and no man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues of the seven angels were fulfilled.

Revelation 16
16:1 And I heard a great voice out of the temple saying to the seven angels, Go your ways, and pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth.
16:2 And the first went, and poured out his vial upon the earth; and there fell a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image.
16:3 And the second angel poured out his vial upon the sea; and it became as the blood of a dead man: and every living soul died in the sea.
16:4 And the third angel poured out his vial upon the rivers and fountains of waters; and they became blood.
16:5 And I heard the angel of the waters say, Thou art righteous, O Lord, which art, and wast, and shalt be, because thou hast judged thus.
16:6 For they have shed the blood of saints and prophets, and thou hast given them blood to drink; for they are worthy.
16:7 And I heard another out of the altar say, Even so, Lord God Almighty, true and righteous are thy judgments.
16:8 And the fourth angel poured out his vial upon the sun; and power was given unto him to scorch men with fire.
16:9 And men were scorched with great heat, and blasphemed the name of God, which hath power over these plagues: and they repented not to give him glory.
16:10 And the fifth angel poured out his vial upon the seat of the beast; and his kingdom was full of darkness; and they gnawed their tongues for pain,
16:11 And blasphemed the God of heaven because of their pains and their sores, and repented not of their deeds.
16:12 And the sixth angel poured out his vial upon the great river Euphrates; and the water thereof was dried up, that the way of the kings of the east might be prepared.
16:13 And I saw three unclean spirits like frogs come out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet.
16:14 For they are the spirits of devils, working miracles, which go forth unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty.
16:15 Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments, lest he walk naked, and they see his shame.
16:16 And he gathered them together into a place called in the Hebrew tongue Armageddon.
16:17 And the seventh angel poured out his vial into the air; and there came a great voice out of the temple of heaven, from the throne, saying, It is done.
16:18 And there were voices, and thunders, and lightnings; and there was a great earthquake, such as was not since men were upon the earth, so mighty an earthquake, and so great.
16:19 And the great city was divided into three parts, and the cities of the nations fell: and great Babylon came in remembrance before God, to give unto her the cup of the wine of the fierceness of his wrath.
16:20 And every island fled away, and the mountains were not found.
16:21 And there fell upon men a great hail out of heaven, every stone about the weight of a talent: and men blasphemed God because of the plague of the hail; for the plague thereof was exceeding great.

Revelation 17
17:1 And there came one of the seven angels which had the seven vials, and talked with me, saying unto me, Come hither; I will shew unto thee the judgment of the great whore that sitteth upon many waters:
17:2 With whom the kings of the earth have committed fornication, and the inhabitants of the earth have been made drunk with the wine of her fornication.
17:3 So he carried me away in the spirit into the wilderness: and I saw a woman sit upon a scarlet coloured beast, full of names of blasphemy, having seven heads and ten horns.
17:4 And the woman was arrayed in purple and scarlet colour, and decked with gold and precious stones and pearls, having a golden cup in her hand full of abominations and filthiness of her fornication:
17:5 And upon her forehead was a name written, MYSTERY, BABYLON THE GREAT, THE MOTHER OF HARLOTS AND ABOMINATIONS OF THE EARTH.
17:6 And I saw the woman drunken with the blood of the saints, and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus: and when I saw her, I wondered with great admiration.
17:7 And the angel said unto me, Wherefore didst thou marvel? I will tell thee the mystery of the woman, and of the beast that carrieth her, which hath the seven heads and ten horns.
17:8 The beast that thou sawest was, and is not; and shall ascend out of the bottomless pit, and go into perdition: and they that dwell on the earth shall wonder, whose names were not written in the book of life from the foundation of the world, when they behold the beast that was, and is not, and yet is.
17:9 And here is the mind which hath wisdom. The seven heads are seven mountains, on which the woman sitteth.
17:10 And there are seven kings: five are fallen, and one is, and the other is not yet come; and when he cometh, he must continue a short space.
17:11 And the beast that was, and is not, even he is the eighth, and is of the seven, and goeth into perdition.
17:12 And the ten horns which thou sawest are ten kings, which have received no kingdom as yet; but receive power as kings one hour with the beast.
17:13 These have one mind, and shall give their power and strength unto the beast.
17:14 These shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings: and they that are with him are called, and chosen, and faithful.
17:15 And he saith unto me, The waters which thou sawest, where the whore sitteth, are peoples, and multitudes, and nations, and tongues.
17:16 And the ten horns which thou sawest upon the beast, these shall hate the whore, and shall make her desolate and naked, and shall eat her flesh, and burn her with fire.
17:17 For God hath put in their hearts to fulfil his will, and to agree, and give their kingdom unto the beast, until the words of God shall be fulfilled.
17:18 And the woman which thou sawest is that great city, which reigneth over the kings of the earth.

Revelation 18
18:1 And after these things I saw another angel come down from heaven, having great power; and the earth was lightened with his glory.
18:2 And he cried mightily with a strong voice, saying, Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen, and is become the habitation of devils, and the hold of every foul spirit, and a cage of every unclean and hateful bird.
18:3 For all nations have drunk of the wine of the wrath of her fornication, and the kings of the earth have committed fornication with her, and the merchants of the earth are waxed rich through the abundance of her delicacies.
18:4 And I heard another voice from heaven, saying, Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins, and that ye receive not of her plagues.
18:5 For her sins have reached unto heaven, and God hath remembered her iniquities.
18:6 Reward her even as she rewarded you, and double unto her double according to her works: in the cup which she hath filled fill to her double.
18:7 How much she hath glorified herself, and lived deliciously, so much torment and sorrow give her: for she saith in her heart, I sit a queen, and am no widow, and shall see no sorrow.
18:8 Therefore shall her plagues come in one day, death, and mourning, and famine; and she shall be utterly burned with fire: for strong is the Lord God who judgeth her.
18:9 And the kings of the earth, who have committed fornication and lived deliciously with her, shall bewail her, and lament for her, when they shall see the smoke of her burning,
18:10 Standing afar off for the fear of her torment, saying, Alas, alas that great city Babylon, that mighty city! for in one hour is thy judgment come.
18:11 And the merchants of the earth shall weep and mourn over her; for no man buyeth their merchandise any more:
18:12 The merchandise of gold, and silver, and precious stones, and of pearls, and fine linen, and purple, and silk, and scarlet, and all thyine wood, and all manner vessels of ivory, and all manner vessels of most precious wood, and of brass, and iron, and marble,
18:13 And cinnamon, and odours, and ointments, and frankincense, and wine, and oil, and fine flour, and wheat, and beasts, and sheep, and horses, and chariots, and slaves, and souls of men.
18:14 And the fruits that thy soul lusted after are departed from thee, and all things which were dainty and goodly are departed from thee, and thou shalt find them no more at all.
18:15 The merchants of these things, which were made rich by her, shall stand afar off for the fear of her torment, weeping and wailing,
18:16 And saying, Alas, alas, that great city, that was clothed in fine linen, and purple, and scarlet, and decked with gold, and precious stones, and pearls!
18:17 For in one hour so great riches is come to nought. And every shipmaster, and all the company in ships, and sailors, and as many as trade by sea, stood afar off,
18:18 And cried when they saw the smoke of her burning, saying, What city is like unto this great city!
18:19 And they cast dust on their heads, and cried, weeping and wailing, saying, Alas, alas, that great city, wherein were made rich all that had ships in the sea by reason of her costliness! for in one hour is she made desolate.
18:20 Rejoice over her, thou heaven, and ye holy apostles and prophets; for God hath avenged you on her.
18:21 And a mighty angel took up a stone like a great millstone, and cast it into the sea, saying, Thus with violence shall that great city Babylon be thrown down, and shall be found no more at all.
18:22 And the voice of harpers, and musicians, and of pipers, and trumpeters, shall be heard no more at all in thee; and no craftsman, of whatsoever craft he be, shall be found any more in thee; and the sound of a millstone shall be heard no more at all in thee;
18:23 And the light of a candle shall shine no more at all in thee; and the voice of the bridegroom and of the bride shall be heard no more at all in thee: for thy merchants were the great men of the earth; for by thy sorceries were all nations deceived.
18:24 And in her was found the blood of prophets, and of saints, and of all that were slain upon the earth.

Revelation 19
19:1 And after these things I heard a great voice of much people in heaven, saying, Alleluia; Salvation, and glory, and honour, and power, unto the Lord our God:
19:2 For true and righteous are his judgments: for he hath judged the great whore, which did corrupt the earth with her fornication, and hath avenged the blood of his servants at her hand.
19:3 And again they said, Alleluia.  And her smoke rose up for ever and ever.
19:4 And the four and twenty elders and the four beasts fell down and worshipped God that sat on the throne, saying, Amen; Alleluia.
19:5 And a voice came out of the throne, saying, Praise our God, all ye his servants, and ye that fear him, both small and great.
19:6 And I heard as it were the voice of a great multitude, and as the voice of many waters, and as the voice of mighty thunderings, saying, Alleluia: for the Lord God omnipotent reigneth.
19:7 Let us be glad and rejoice, and give honour to him: for the marriage of the Lamb is come, and his wife hath made herself ready.
19:8 And to her was granted that she should be arrayed in fine linen, clean and white: for the fine linen is the righteousness of saints.
19:9 And he saith unto me, Write, Blessed are they which are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb. And he saith unto me, These are the true sayings of God.
19:10 And I fell at his feet to worship him. And he said unto me, See thou do it not: I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren that have the testimony of Jesus: worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy.
19:11 And I saw heaven opened, and behold a white horse; and he that sat upon him was called Faithful and True, and in righteousness he doth judge and make war.
19:12 His eyes were as a flame of fire, and on his head were many crowns; and he had a name written, that no man knew, but he himself.
19:13 And he was clothed with a vesture dipped in blood: and his name is called The Word of God.
19:14 And the armies which were in heaven followed him upon white horses, clothed in fine linen, white and clean.
19:15 And out of his mouth goeth a sharp sword, that with it he should smite the nations: and he shall rule them with a rod of iron: and he treadeth the winepress of the fierceness and wrath of Almighty God.
19:16 And he hath on his vesture and on his thigh a name written, KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS.
19:17 And I saw an angel standing in the sun; and he cried with a loud voice, saying to all the fowls that fly in the midst of heaven, Come and gather yourselves together unto the supper of the great God;
19:18 That ye may eat the flesh of kings, and the flesh of captains, and the flesh of mighty men, and the flesh of horses, and of them that sit on them, and the flesh of all men, both free and bond, both small and great.
19:19 And I saw the beast, and the kings of the earth, and their armies, gathered together to make war against him that sat on the horse, and against his army.
19:20 And the beast was taken, and with him the false prophet that wrought miracles before him, with which he deceived them that had received the mark of the beast, and them that worshipped his image. These both were cast alive into a lake of fire burning with brimstone.
19:21 And the remnant were slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse, which sword proceeded out of his mouth: and all the fowls were filled with their flesh.

Revelation 20
20:1 And I saw an angel come down from heaven, having the key of the bottomless pit and a great chain in his hand.
20:2 And he laid hold on the dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil, and Satan, and bound him a thousand years,
20:3 And cast him into the bottomless pit, and shut him up, and set a seal upon him, that he should deceive the nations no more, till the thousand years should be fulfilled: and after that he must be loosed a little season.
20:4 And I saw thrones, and they sat upon them, and judgment was given unto them: and I saw the souls of them that were beheaded for the witness of Jesus, and for the word of God, and which had not worshipped the beast, neither his image, neither had received his mark upon their foreheads, or in their hands; and they lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years.
20:5 But the rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished. This is the first resurrection.
20:6 Blessed and holy is he that hath part in the first resurrection: on such the second death hath no power, but they shall be priests of God and of Christ, and shall reign with him a thousand years.
20:7 And when the thousand years are expired, Satan shall be loosed out of his prison,
20:8 And shall go out to deceive the nations which are in the four quarters of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them together to battle: the number of whom is as the sand of the sea.
20:9 And they went up on the breadth of the earth, and compassed the camp of the saints about, and the beloved city: and fire came down from God out of heaven, and devoured them.
20:10 And the devil that deceived them was cast into the lake of fire and brimstone, where the beast and the false prophet are, and shall be tormented day and night for ever and ever.
20:11 And I saw a great white throne, and him that sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away; and there was found no place for them.
20:12 And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works.
20:13 And the sea gave up the dead which were in it; and death and hell delivered up the dead which were in them: and they were judged every man according to their works.
20:14 And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death.
20:15 And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.

Revelation 21
21:1 And I saw a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the first earth were passed away; and there was no more sea.
21:2 And I John saw the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down from God out of heaven, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband.
21:3 And I heard a great voice out of heaven saying, Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them, and they shall be his people, and God himself shall be with them, and be their God.
21:4 And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away.
21:5 And he that sat upon the throne said, Behold, I make all things new. And he said unto me, Write: for these words are true and faithful.
21:6 And he said unto me, It is done. I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end. I will give unto him that is athirst of the fountain of the water of life freely.
21:7 He that overcometh shall inherit all things; and I will be his God, and he shall be my son.
21:8 But the fearful, and unbelieving, and the abominable, and murderers, and whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, shall have their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is the second death.
21:9 And there came unto me one of the seven angels which had the seven vials full of the seven last plagues, and talked with me, saying, Come hither, I will shew thee the bride, the Lamb’s wife.
21:10 And he carried me away in the spirit to a great and high mountain, and shewed me that great city, the holy Jerusalem, descending out of heaven from God,
21:11 Having the glory of God: and her light was like unto a stone most precious, even like a jasper stone, clear as crystal;
21:12 And had a wall great and high, and had twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and names written thereon, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel:
21:13 On the east three gates; on the north three gates; on the south three gates; and on the west three gates.
21:14 And the wall of the city had twelve foundations, and in them the names of the twelve apostles of the Lamb.
21:15 And he that talked with me had a golden reed to measure the city, and the gates thereof, and the wall thereof.
21:16 And the city lieth foursquare, and the length is as large as the breadth: and he measured the city with the reed, twelve thousand furlongs. The length and the breadth and the height of it are equal.
21:17 And he measured the wall thereof, an hundred and forty and four cubits, according to the measure of a man, that is, of the angel.
21:18 And the building of the wall of it was of jasper: and the city was pure gold, like unto clear glass.
21:19 And the foundations of the wall of the city were garnished with all manner of precious stones. The first foundation was jasper; the second, sapphire; the third, a chalcedony; the fourth, an emerald;
21:20 The fifth, sardonyx; the sixth, sardius; the seventh, chrysolyte; the eighth, beryl; the ninth, a topaz; the tenth, a chrysoprasus; the eleventh, a jacinth; the twelfth, an amethyst.
21:21 And the twelve gates were twelve pearls: every several gate was of one pearl: and the street of the city was pure gold, as it were transparent glass.
21:22 And I saw no temple therein: for the Lord God Almighty and the Lamb are the temple of it.
21:23 And the city had no need of the sun, neither of the moon, to shine in it: for the glory of God did lighten it, and the Lamb is the light thereof.
21:24 And the nations of them which are saved shall walk in the light of it: and the kings of the earth do bring their glory and honour into it.
21:25 And the gates of it shall not be shut at all by day: for there shall be no night there.
21:26 And they shall bring the glory and honour of the nations into it.
21:27 And there shall in no wise enter into it any thing that defileth, neither whatsoever worketh abomination, or maketh a lie: but they which are written in the Lamb’s book of life.

Revelation 22
22:1 And he shewed me a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb.
22:2 In the midst of the street of it, and on either side of the river, was there the tree of life, which bare twelve manner of fruits, and yielded her fruit every month: and the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations.
22:3 And there shall be no more curse: but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him:
22:4 And they shall see his face; and his name shall be in their foreheads.
22:5 And there shall be no night there; and they need no candle, neither light of the sun; for the Lord God giveth them light: and they shall reign for ever and ever.
22:6 And he said unto me, These sayings are faithful and true: and the Lord God of the holy prophets sent his angel to shew unto his servants the things which must shortly be done.
22:7 Behold, I come quickly: blessed is he that keepeth the sayings of the prophecy of this book.
22:8 And I John saw these things, and heard them. And when I had heard and seen, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel which shewed me these things.
22:9 Then saith he unto me, See thou do it not: for I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren the prophets, and of them which keep the sayings of this book: worship God.
22:10 And he saith unto me, Seal not the sayings of the prophecy of this book: for the time is at hand.
22:11 He that is unjust, let him be unjust still: and he which is filthy, let him be filthy still: and he that is righteous, let him be righteous still: and he that is holy, let him be holy still.
22:12 And, behold, I come quickly; and my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be.
22:13 I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end, the first and the last.
22:14 Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city.
22:15 For without are dogs, and sorcerers, and whoremongers, and murderers, and idolaters, and whosoever loveth and maketh a lie.
22:16 I Jesus have sent mine angel to testify unto you these things in the churches. I am the root and the offspring of David, and the bright and morning star.
22:17 And the Spirit and the bride say, Come. And let him that heareth say, Come. And let him that is athirst come. And whosoever will, let him take the water of life freely.
22:18 For I testify unto every man that heareth the words of the prophecy of this book, If any man shall add unto these things, God shall add unto him the plagues that are written in this book:
22:19 And if any man shall take away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God shall take away his part out of the book of life, and out of the holy city, and from the things which are written in this book.
22:20 He which testifieth these things saith, Surely I come quickly. Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus.
22:21 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen.
